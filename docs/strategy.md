This is a partially observed economy. Remote books may be hidden, NPC orders requote, players consume liquidity, and future hazards are uncertain. No strategy can prove global maximum profit from the public information. The objective is the best executable, conservatively valued opportunity among observed candidates, followed by measured reinvestment. Every decision must distinguish an actual quote from an estimate.

## Game 0.5.1

The [guide, hazards and golden rules](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) describes variable storm losses. Most storms are light. Rough water and an under-rated hull make heavier losses more likely. A pirate raid that releases the ship takes 20% to 50% of each cargo stack and its stores. Route `safe_provisions_tons` covers the worst storm on any leg, not the worst pirate raid. Keep the hard hull-rating check and buy safe stores.

## Units and clock

All monetary calculations use integer pence: 100 pence = 1 Crown. Freight rewards are per contract; charter rewards are per delivered ton. Rates are basis points. Ship speed is centiknots. The current world clock reports its acceleration; at time scale 60, one game day takes 24 real minutes. Profit rates, operating costs, and payback use game days. HTTP timeouts, diagnostic retention, and the server's 24-hour idempotency window use real time.

Server timestamps, not the workstation clock, govern berth unlocks and contract deadlines. A fresh clock fetch supplies a conservative interval from its request start, completion, and `real_utc`; unlocks use its lower bound and deadline admission uses its upper bound. The acceleration applies when converting elapsed real time to game durations, not a second time to server UTC. HTTP cooldowns and replay windows remain host-time deadlines.

## Comparing opportunities

Admit each shipment on its own destination-ending contribution and time to delivery:

`destination net pence = realizable sale/reward - acquisition - buyer customs - consumed-store replacement - attributable harbour bills and upkeep`

`net pence per game day = destination net pence / delivery duration`

Full safe-store inventory and collateral still have to fit the hold and funded cash budget. Their principal is not all consumed expense. Loading, unloading, berth locks, waiting and loaded sailing speed remain part of delivery duration. Ranking then considers onward work or a priced ballast opportunity cost as described below. This does not commit an empty return. Capacity growth uses its separate repeatable earning forecast after incumbent reservations; a continuation ranking score is not debt-service proof.

Evaluate quantities at actual order-book depth boundaries, available cash, hold capacity, contract demand, and deadline constraints. Buying the full hold can cross into an unprofitable ask level. Selling at the best bid is valid only for the quantity actually supported at that bid. Never treat NPC stock as buy-side demand. Exclude identifiable own-player orders.

Use actual integer customs and fees, conservatively rounding where required. Customs apply to purchases, not to the seller's proceeds. Record actual filled quantity and actual costs from IOC responses; a successful response may still fill zero or only part of the request.

Serial execution and fresh reads between ships prevent the fleet from repeatedly allocating the same cash or liquidity. Unresolved mutations block dependent spending until reconciled.

## Spot, charter, and freight distinctions

### Spot trading

Use complete accessible depth. Gossip has a source time and weaker visibility: it is not a fresh executable book and does not imply an arbitrary volume of demand. Discard unsupported capacity assumptions. A quote becoming visible again does not make an old observation new.

### Event-aware revenue and calibration

The [event pricing policy](../internal/engine/market_events.go) uses temporary world events for each destination and commodity. Structural changes are not temporary reversions. Compare observations and bounded server-time arrivals with both overlapping shocks and intervening transitions. A pre-transition quote crossing a `supply_boom` or another potentially receipt-lowering shock must survive a further 25% revenue stress, even if it predates the start by seconds. Use the later of the current server upper bound and arrival to detect that transition, never host time. Known bid-supporting `demand_shortage` and `admiralty_mobilization` shocks keep the safe aged pre-event fallback. If bids were observed within a shock, unknown arrival or arrival after expiry cannot use those shock bids: use eligible retained pre-event evidence, still subject to the adverse-transition rule, or reject that revenue basis. In-window shock bids also require the 25% stress. Missing or stale event authority applies the same stress to recent observations within 30 real minutes. These are controller risk rules, not measured loss probabilities. Pre-event bids retain commodity-local server observation times, shock-overlapping samples do not teach ordinary bid drift, and other inbound lots consume fresh as well as remembered destination depth.

The [revenue calibration](../internal/store/settlements.go) groups receipts by `revenue_basis`. Persisted `_revenue_calibration_since` marks the pricing-regime cutover. Both mission start and completion must be on or after it. Use the latest 20 eligible known-cost commercial settlements per basis, excluding unknown proceeds, positive cargo shortfalls, mixed cargo/freight receipts and partial IOC purchases whose quoted quantity no longer matches the purchased lot. Mixed settlements retain their combined receipts in history, but their fixed rewards cannot dilute the cargo calibration. The settlement stores the uncalibrated gross-revenue quote; legacy or unpriced plans without that quote cannot calibrate prices. Planned net remains the calibrated admission estimate. Fewer than ten samples leaves revenue unchanged. Otherwise take the median actual-to-uncalibrated gross-revenue ratio and clamp it to 90% through 100%. Scale cargo receipts, not any fixed freight reward, costs or net profit. If referenced freight terms are unavailable, leave revenue uncalibrated and persist the reason in the plan. Event stress likewise applies only to cargo receipts. The factor and boundary survive restart; old bad event pricing cannot seed a new regime.

The [two-leg ranker](../internal/engine/competition.go) forecasts onward work only for the top three admitted first legs with fresh destination quay depth, sharing one forecast per quay without recursion. Its bounded onward shortlist compares cargo and freight after voyage costs; only cost-dominated one-ton rejections remain eligible for larger-lot sizing. It preserves observation age and applies the first-leg delay; it does not invent a fresh future book. Admission-only growth and hazard lookups skip onward forecasts. If no supported onward trade exists, a priced ballast leg shares its return time, provisions and harbour-cost calculation with the repeat forecast. Rank by `(first net + onward net) / (first days + onward days)` when available, otherwise use the first-leg rate. This is an estimate, not a promised voyage. Competition breaks close economic choices even when every combined score is nonpositive. Computed continuation-rank authority survives plan persistence, and funding fees refresh both single-leg and combined scores without marking standalone plans as continuation-ranked. Capacity therefore retains their separately costed return voyage. Departure and later purchases still require fresh admission. Replay frames retain the same calibration and active-marque inputs as live planning.


### Retained quay books

The [guide, visibility tiers](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) limits live depth to quay presence. The controller therefore keeps our last complete quay bids after departure. These are aged estimates, not current access or guaranteed demand. [Retention and pricing rules](../internal/engine/book_memory.go) keep at most 10 bid levels per commodity for 12 game days and remove our own orders. Failed captures keep the last good entry.

Fresh full depth wins, then sufficiently recent player-bid gossip with enough quantity, then retained bids, then the one-ton gossip probe. Other unsettled inbound lots consume retained bids first. Fresher lower bids cap remembered prices. Drift samples need at least 0.5 game days between captures and exclude our own fills. The age haircut is `ceil(age in game days × drift bps per day)`. The drift floor is 100 bps per day; after 30 valid samples it can rise to the measured adverse 75th percentile. Retain at most 200 drift samples. Reject a haircut above 6,000 bps. Additional price stress uses `min(6,000, 2 × haircut + 300)` bps, less the age haircut already charged. A retained lot needs at least one penny after that additional stress and the ordinary minimum margin. Its revenue basis is `retained`; it does not establish depth-backed debt income.

### Observed competition

The rolling 24-real-hour competition summary is cached for five real minutes. Cache misses use recorded events, a small owner-name projection and the runner's in-memory snapshot, without display credential decryption or extra game requests. Counts are distinct observed foreign company/ship-name identities in a destination or yard's banking region, not a census or live ship locations. Partial observations, including an observed zero, never establish an empty region. The runner persists continuous fresh quay-observation spans; a region watched for less than the full 24-hour identity window, or with a polling gap over two minutes, stays neutral. Such short coverage does not set either end of the observed range. Missing or short coverage uses the midpoint of eligible regional ship counts, not zero; with no eligible observed spread, competition has no ranking effect. A failed load clears the signal, reports coverage as unavailable and emits at most one warning per five-minute cache interval while economic planning continues.

Among accepted voyages whose ranking score is at least 95% of the economic best, prefer fewer observed foreign ships. The tolerance is anchored to the best candidate, not chained through pairwise comparisons. All safety, funding and profitability decisions remain unchanged. Capacity builds retain the competition evidence for their best repeatable earning lane, not merely their build yard; equal earning rates prefer lower initial store capital first, using competition only if stores are equal, so competition cannot change admission. Funded capacity candidates within 5% of the best net return on hull, gun and store capital prefer the lower-competition earning region. Cash-blocked financing candidates apply the same comparison using depth-backed earnings and their own earning-region evidence. The separate proven-lane growth path uses competition only at equal capital returns and equal daily net.

Expansion yards rank positive observed stressed spot spreads first; yards within 5% of the best spread prefer lower observed regional competition. World phase is an unlock boundary, not evidence of fewer traders. Never reject a voyage or build solely because another company has many ships. Comparable or selected candidate details include region, coverage, observed companies/ships and the neutral ranking value; unknown counts remain null. Rolling competition details are excluded from decision fingerprints, so changing traffic alone does not defeat diagnostic deduplication.

### Expansion and private charters

Charter delivery occurs at the contract's `origin_port_id`. Only goods carried in from a different port qualify. A local purchase cannot be immediately converted into a charter reward. Track evidenced voyages and eligible quantities; unknown imported history is not proof of eligibility. Exclude stores and sealed freight. Cap delivery by current remaining demand and compare the charter payout with actual executable market proceeds.

Public charters are first-come demand. Check their remaining quantity and expiry before purchase and again before delivery. If demand closes en route, evaluate the actual alternate market rather than pretending the original reward is guaranteed.

### Freight

Freight cargo is sealed and its collateral is tied-up capital, not an expense unless lost. Preserve each accepted obligation, including returned delivery deadline, carrier, destination, quantity, reward, and collateral. Do not count a collateral refund as income. Do not sell, consume, unload, or charter-deliver sealed cargo.

The acceptance deadline is authoritative; listing expiry is not the delivery deadline. Check active and terminal contracts explicitly because listing defaults may show only open offers. A refused delivery can still record a tender. Preserve that obligation and its waiting state instead of treating every 409 as a harmless no-op. Include waiting provisions in the budget.

Compare cargo and compatible freight together before rejecting either component: shared sailing and harbour costs can make the combination profitable even when one alone cannot carry the trip. Recheck remaining quantities and seal occupancy; never allocate the same capacity or cash twice. After a purchase has filled, compare future departure cash with actual executable local recovery. Paid acquisition is sunk for that choice but remains in the final realized profit/loss record.

## Sailing and idle costs

Reject any route whose edge or traversed zone exceeds the ship's hull rating. Check every leg, not just the destination. An attractive silver margin is irrelevant if the route is not legal for the hull.

Use route `safe_provisions_tons`, not the optimistic expected requirement, plus the appropriate port waiting allowance. Crew consume provisions while berthed too. Re-read stores before departure; a partial provisioning fill is not permission to sail. Guns consume hold space and newly built ships do not arrive ready to sail.

Berth handling imposes a minimum 30-game-minute lock, with extra handling time per ton. A departure refusal after a successful purchase must schedule a fresh departure attempt after unlock, never repeat the purchase. Port dues have a minimum six-game-hour berth charge. Anchored roadstead can avoid berth costs but does not erase provisioning needs.

Idle replenishment owns a durable maintenance intent. Roadstead observations may hide executable provisions asks, so one dock can be necessary to inspect the quay. A missing or unaffordable quote then retains the intent instead of repeatedly docking and going offshore. The wait is not free: berth dues and crew upkeep remain protected obligations.

At the quay, the controller quotes the whole residual store requirement at current depth, including buyer customs and conservative integer rounding. If local cash is insufficient, measured regional fees and donor surpluses must cover the complete requirement without consuming any incumbent commitment or operating reserve. Only one immutable remittance is staged at a time; every receipt is followed by fresh pricing and funding checks. Invalidating a never-sent order does not release its still-needed funded cash.

Standalone idle replenishment can use stock claimed only by another unsubmitted idle quote; otherwise shrinking depth can make both ships wait forever. Serialized execution persists the winner's request before another consumer runs. Every attached request keeps its depth claim, other economic consumers still reserve maintenance depth, and all cash commitments remain protected. Held maintenance is reusable fleet capacity, not justification to buy a replacement.

Partial fills, refusals and lost docking receipts resume maintenance; confirmed sufficient stores retire it rather than wait for optional guns. Gun-only docking checks refit affordability and hold capacity first. Mandatory commissioning armament is unchanged. Actual transfer fees and store purchases are expenses, never the transferred principal.

Sound-toll routes are always rejected until an authoritative toll-cost observation source exists. The [guide, marine insurance](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) publishes Lloyd's expected-claim upper bound through `GET /v1/insurance/quote`. New voyages charge that bound against purchase cost, buyer customs and freight collateral at risk. It is not a measured probability. A zone's storm rating is not a substitute. Diagnostic stress remains separate from this expected-net charge.

The [hazard quote controller](../internal/engine/hazard.go) ranks fetches by candidate economics, with deferred pairs ahead on the next pass and primary quotes ahead of laden refinements. It sends at most 30 quote GETs per refresh. Direct hull quotes last 6 game hours; laden lane quotes last 30 game days. Changes in cannons, calendar month or route-zone net piracy invalidate normal reuse. Pricing takes the higher applicable bound. An owned-hull quote on the same lane can serve as a labelled proxy only when its cannon count and hull-category rank are no greater than the priced ship's. Use the highest eligible bound. The quote's piracy observations must cover current route-zone piracy. After a failed refresh, a published eligible lane bound can remain a labelled stale proxy for up to 90 game days, but only within the same winter or nonwinter storm season. Winter is November through February. A first-ever lane during an outage stays unpriced and is rejected, never assigned zero risk.

The [minimum-margin rule](../internal/engine/planner.go) requires hazard-adjusted expected net of at least 2% of purchase plus buyer customs for spot, charter and mixed freight loads. Pure freight needs at least one penny. This is controller policy for model error, not a game tax or measured loss rate. The [guide, core loop](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) requires comparison after customs, spread and voyage costs.

Already-paid funding fees are sunk when [rechecking continuation](../internal/engine/income_funding.go). Remaining net must be nonnegative once those fees are added back. Do not apply the 2% admission margin again to an already-funded voyage. The paid fees remain an expense in its final result.

Live calibration exposed why this distinction matters: the initial Le Havre to Portsmouth provision charter had a modeled 36,055-pence cash margin for 24 tons, but an unsupported 14.5% revenue haircut rejected it. Those prices are a dated observation, not a standing trade signal. Every order must still use refreshed depth and current charter terms.

The offline planner exposes `NextRungReceiptStress`, disabled in the live Runner. When enabled, a fresh-quay spot lot must remain net-positive after repricing its full receipt at the next lower NPC bid rung, including observed funding fees. Its IOC sell limit still follows the worst actually consumed bid, not the stress price. Charters, freight and gossip retain their current economics. This is an experiment, not an adopted live policy: compare loss-inclusive simulated returns and idle ship time across all three regeneration scenarios before seeking the owner's approval. No measured improvement from this gate has been established; already-paid cargo continuation is unchanged.

### Destination replanning and bounded sourcing

New economic voyages settle at their delivery destination once cargo is empty, all sealed obligations are terminal, turnaround has elapsed, and authoritative offshore state is observed. They then compare opportunities from that port instead of automatically sailing empty to their original source. Previously persisted roundtrip missions retain their original return commitments and accounting.

When no ordinary executable opportunity exists, an empty, stationary ship may inspect another source for an open charter. Compare at most three source/charter pairs using catalog production or fresh public ask gossip, legal routes and conservative deadline slack. Catalog production is evidence worth investigating, not an executable inventory promise; an unstocked local manufacturer does not veto a better evidenced remote source. Public evidence never grants private market access or creates forecast sale revenue.

The [rolling probe budget](../internal/engine/probe_history.go) replaces the lifetime seed and larger idle allowance. Over the last 60 game days, use `clamp(20,000 + floor(conversion net / 2) - probe expenses, 0, floor(free cash / 50))`, in pence. Free cash excludes operating reserves and all current commitments. Conversion net is known-cost delivery net from a source probed no more than seven real days before that delivery started. Losses count too. Scout expenses do not consume this budget. Unknown probe costs in the window close it. The bounded conversion ledger retains 200 rows; the accounting record remains authoritative.

After six coherent idle game hours, the controller may reinspect unchanged evidence, subject to the same rolling budget and a 24-real-minute matching completion cooldown. A future completion blocks it. Maintenance, docking, selection and remittance do not reset physical idle time. Sailing, carried cargo, active freight or a port change do. Stale, failed, paused or regressed clocks earn no idle time. These are [controller spending rules](../internal/engine/sourcing.go), not forecast sales. The [guide, visibility](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) still requires arrival and a fresh quay inspection before buying.

Offline experiments may replace the per-probe cash share with `SourcingCashBPS`; nil and 200 retain the live 2% share, while zero disables sourcing's cash allowance. The rolling conversion budget still caps every share. The live Runner supplies neither experiment knob.

A source/commodity with an actual conversion in the last seven real days is exempt from the 24-hour ban, but not the existing lane checks. After two nonconverting probes, the ship cannot start another catalogue-only probe until it departs laden or earns six additional coherent idle game hours.

After 12 coherent idle game hours, a ship with no accepted ordinary voyage or charter relocation may scout an unlocked port in ballast. ## Treasury and reinvestment

Cash is regional. Escrow is not spendable. Reserve enforcement includes outstanding obligations, collateral, near-term bills, and working capital in the billing region. Any negative vault, or any underfunded active/obligated region, blocks discretionary construction while safe income voyages continue. Empty unused regions need no artificial reserve. A remittance pays an observed fee and must preserve its source's protected funds. Several donors can fund a shortfall through sequential partial transfers; each durable transfer is followed by fresh balances before selecting the next donor.

Ordinary positive-contribution voyages can draw on reserve-safe donor cash instead of being limited to their origin vault. Quote the full immutable cargo-order bound, customs, safe-store cash, collateral, fleet bills and destination funding together; pure freight does not invent an ordinary-cargo purchase. Include observed remittance fees once in destination net, stress and repeat forecasts. The ship mission owns each sequential transfer, so company treasury work cannot pre-fund a merely conditional destination. Revalidate the pinned opportunity before funding or procurement and again at first send after rate-limit waits. If it disappears before cargo is acquired, settle paid fees as an aborted voyage rather than deleting them.

Once spot cargo is paid for, the ship compares sailing on against selling it locally. A destination quay book goes stale as soon as no ship remains there to refresh it, which is usually right after buying for it. Holding paid cargo in port until new evidence appears only accrues dues. For ordinary non-event cargo without a fresh destination book, the tons aboard are valued at the per-ton sell floor accepted at purchase (worst depth fill, stressed NPC bid or one-ton probe floor; never freight income), lowered by any fresh gossip floor. A fresh but thin book counts only the receipts that fill now. A fully executable local exit that beats this value after bills still liquidates locally. This valuation never authorizes a new purchase, and the arrival sale still walks live bids without a price floor.

An admitted event quote is not a permanent sell floor. [Paid-cargo recovery](../internal/engine/procurement.go) compares executable local receipts after conservative extra handling with a usable, arrival-aged retained/pre-event destination estimate after the new destination stay and consumed-store replacement. Local recovery at or above that estimated net selects liquidation, even below acquisition cost: paid acquisition is sunk and stays in the realized profit/loss record, not the comparison threshold. Only when no usable destination estimate exists does actual known acquisition cost supply a conservative fallback floor. Below that floor, with only partial local liquidity, or with neither an estimate nor known acquisition cost, continue to the original destination under the existing route, stores and freight safety gates.

Stationary harbour obligations follow actual presence; sailing obligations follow the final authoritative route leg, not the departed origin retained in a ship record. A prospective destination still needs its configured reserve floor and the new arrival bill even when no current hull obligates that region. These are liquidity protections, not additional voyage expenses.

The guide's daily fleet upkeep includes a nonlinear coastal/regional component:

`45 Cr × (max(0, coastal/regional tons + floor(oceanic tons / 2) - 40) / 100)^1.45`

It also includes `120 Cr × factors^1.25` and `1 Cr × oceanic ton`. The [guide, treasury and daily bills](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) gives these terms. [DailyUpkeep](../internal/engine/planner.go) rounds the combined nonlinear charge upward in pence. Use the marginal change from adding a hull or factor, not a standalone or fleet-average cost.

The cash order is operating obligations and debt service first, then admissible cheap-hull investment or its bounded savings target, then surplus Goldsmiths prepayment. [Prepayment](../internal/engine/loans.go) requires fresh debt and cash authority, no growth intent or live savings target, and all regional reserves funded. A capacity hold for an unrelated reason does not block it. In the loan's region, keep those reserves plus `(non-sunk fleet size + 1) × cargo-cash lot`. The repaid loan's own installment reserve is released by the payoff, so it does not count against the floor. The payment budgets the observed balance plus one game day of interest and omits the amount, so the server settles the exact balance at execution. Only pay an active owned non-mortgage loan when that budget fits above the floor. First-send checks repeat this protection. It does not remit just to prepay, drain cargo float, or manually prepay mortgages. Scheduled debt service still runs ahead of growth.


The lane-proof growth path requires:

1. At least two positive known-cost delivered cycles and positive loss-inclusive account and lane net in the last 12 recorded host-UTC hours. Include complete qualifying cycles, in-window failures, losses and probes; unknown costs or unmeasured duration disqualify the proof. The interval is inclusive and a relevant future completion vetoes evaluation. This is a host-recorded recency policy, not a guarantee about server elapsed time. Older losses and uncertainty remain in permanent history; current unresolved actions, missions and liabilities remain protected regardless of age.
2. Two cycles of observed source asks after competing source/commodity repeat throughput, plus each incumbent's not-yet-purchased lot once. Spot trades likewise need two cycles of executable destination bids plus undelivered competing lots once.
3. For finite charters, remaining quantity of at least `outstanding undelivered lots + max(2, ceil(payback / cycle) + 1) × (new lot + competing contract repeat throughput per cycle)`, with expiry no earlier than payback plus one complete cycle. Future reposts and market requotes do not guarantee replacement demand.
4. A legal route for the hull and seasonal conditions.
5. A fresh available shipyard quote with a 10% price-slippage buffer.
6. Incumbent working capital, a reusable new trading float, full initial safe-store inventory, guns and regional reserves funded separately. Price initial provisions against executable asks including buyer customs and integer rounding; consumption replacement value is not the commissioning budget. Protect destination arrival bills and three days of marginal upkeep in their actual billing regions.
7. A payback within the configured game-day horizon. Compare feasible quantities across supported hulls and accessible yards, rather than selecting the first passing package. Rank incremental net per day against all newly committed regional cash plus funding fees, including new reserve floors, not just the hull price. Existing reserves are not charged again. There is no ship-count cap; recalculate depth, marginal upkeep and cash after every purchase.

Shipyard quotes are exact observations, not binding price locks: the build endpoint has no quote token or maximum-price precondition. Before its first send, cash-funded construction must still have current shipyard access, an available quote, sufficient executable demand and the full commissioning and incumbent funding protections above. Financed construction uses the frozen admission and live checks in [Capacity financing](#capacity-financing). Purchases, provisions, refits and collateral likewise require current financial authority and funded reserves. Already attempted requests retain their immutable replay identity instead of being repriced as new spending. Settle the actual build result and treat build, refit, and provision as separate durable actions.

Allocate source, destination and contract reservations only to actual competing lanes, including committed sailing returns; unrelated hulls do not consume every lane's liquidity. Repeat throughput uses each committed lot and full cycle duration. Whole outstanding lots are reserved once, not once per future cycle; already delivered ordinary cargo on a returning ship consumes no remaining contract or bid capacity. Delivering sealed freight does not release ordinary trade reservations on a mixed voyage. Run actionable maintenance before sizing an idle hull's economic plan because guns reduce its hold; a hull without a local yard or funds for a refit must not veto otherwise viable construction. Assign compatible idle or newly commissioned capacity before buying another hull. One durable `__growth` mission controls construction, refit and provisioning across restarts.

Commissioning stores and funding fees remain operating costs through handoff, abort or hull loss; they cannot disappear when the growth control is deleted. Preserve unknown cost state rather than manufacturing profit. Routine planning and first-send procurement use the same residual quantities as growth, so an ordinary ship cannot spend a returning ship's reserved next lot. Re-price changing dues without waiting for the quote to stop changing.

The player market supports voluntary ship listings and purchases through `/v1/fleet/list-ship` and `/v1/fleet/buy-ship`. The engine does not use either endpoint: a purchased hull retains its seller's name, and there is no agreed exception to the Pokemon naming rule. Do not count hypothetical resale proceeds or deliberately trigger bailiff seizure to finance expansion.

### Measured capacity growth

The [capacity controller](../internal/engine/growth_capacity.go) buys only hoy, cutter, brigantine and collier hulls. Barque, fluyt, frigate and packet cannot become capacity candidates or savings targets. It needs accepted repeatable non-charter work after incumbent commitments. Future-dated settlement evidence vetoes every hull.

Cash-funded cheap hulls do not wait for a measured earnings cohort. Their accepted work, marginal upkeep, cash, safe stores and payback checks still apply. Mortgages require measured net from surviving owned hulls of the same class over the trailing 30 game days. Clip each hull's age to that window. Missing ages, unknown costs, future completions or fewer than 24 class hull-game-days leave this evidence unavailable. Measured net per class hull-game-day must cover `1.25 × quoted installment / 10`. The used earning rate is the lower of predicted and measured rate, less marginal upkeep and quoted daily interest. Retained books cannot supply the executable depth required for ordinary capacity loans. These are controller rules, not game guarantees.

Its fare floor covers the whole group's modeled passage cost, priced ballast repositioning and Lloyd's bound. Never offer below it.

Start at the floor when no more than 3 game hours remain before `board_by`; a delayed replacement may miss that floor before boarding closes. Cancel and replacement are separate journaled writes. After boarding, require a matching durable passage and safe stores, not a renewed on-time promise check. Lateness reduces fare; holding aboard groups indefinitely only makes it worse. ### Capacity saving

Buying the best affordable hull now is not always the fastest way to grow fleet earnings. Compare that hull A with every economically viable, cash-blocked hull B supported by repeatable executable depth. B must outrank A under the ordinary capacity return-on-capital rule using both its build-time earning net and its depth-backed net, with the same best-anchored 5% competition band for each comparison. A higher absolute income alone is not enough. A yard whose only presence is a temporary expansion agent cannot become a savings target.

Measure the fleet's savings rate R in pence per game day from the last 24 real hours: divide recorded settlement `NetPence + UnknownLossPence` by `window seconds × time_scale / 86400`, then subtract one tenth of each active amortised borrower loan's installment. Settlement expenses already include claimed upkeep bills, so do not deduct fleet-and-factor upkeep again. Recorded charters count toward R, even though they never support B's repeat income. Unknown-cost losses count negatively; routine unknown-cost rows do not switch saving off. The existing future-completion hold still applies. A nonpositive R disables saving.

Let Ca be A's hull price, quote cushion, guns and initial stores; ra its incremental net per game day; Sb B's current total cash shortfall; rb B's depth-backed incremental net; and L one additional cargo-cash lot. Compare both purchase orders:

- `tSave = Sb / R`
- `tBuyThenB = (Sb + Ca + L) / (R + ra)`
- `tSaveThenA = tSave + (Ca + L) / (R + rb)`
- `margin = rb × (tBuyThenB - tSave) - ra × tSaveThenA`

Save only for a strictly positive margin and a positive shortfall, and only when `tSave <= 24 real hours × time_scale / 24` game days, which is 60 game days at scale 60. Choose the B with the largest positive margin. Equality buys A now. Try normal capacity financing for that B only when `(rb + ra) × tSave` exceeds estimated total interest, `36 × installment - principal`; otherwise waiting earns more than the avoided wait costs. A financing refusal retains the cash instead of building A. An affordable A is not deferred to finance a B outside this bounded savings rule. Once B is affordable, prefer it unless A wins the same ranking with an earning net at least as high as B's; equality does not justify paying more capital for the same earnings.

The durable `_capacity_savings_target` records the yard, hull, A-independent required cash, capital, depth net and depth competition preference. Required cash is current available cash plus B's evaluated shortfall, including only fees accounted for by that evaluation. Its real-UTC expiry is the evaluation time plus `min(maxWait, 1.25 × tSave) × 86400 / time_scale` seconds: 25% slack for a noisy savings estimate, still bounded by the measured history window. A fresh winning evaluation may refresh its economics and shorten its expiry, but cannot extend the same yard/hull's deadline. A still-viable cash-blocked B retains its target when no affordable A exists. Fresh unavailability, mismatched quote identity, a nonviable or losing evaluation, or a construction backoff drops it; expiry and a completed build drop it too. Missing or stale quotes/books, request cooldowns and non-quay observations leave the yard unevaluated and do not clear the target. Losing yard presence alone does not release the cash: an affordable capacity hull or lane-proof package must beat the live target by the same window-bounded comparison. Once B is affordable without presence, hold only while a ship's current voyage or committed mission ends at that yard; otherwise release the target rather than wait without a route back.

An expired yard/hull is recorded in `_capacity_savings_expired` and cannot restart a savings hold until another build completes or 24 real hours pass, whichever comes first. This exclusion survives a pass with no affordable A and a process restart; expiration must release the original deadline rather than silently begin another wait for the same hull.

While saving, `_capacity_growth_port` stays empty, expansion-agent hiring waits, and new underwriting collateral and marque purchases wait. An existing expansion agent at the saved yard remains needed even before a build mission exists. Existing ships continue earning and servicing obligations. `capacity_savings:<port>|<hull>` records rounded game days and whole-Crown rates and capital, with the hold reason `capacity growth saves for a better hull`.

A feasible lane package compares against B's stored economics while B remains cash-blocked. Fresh negative quote observations still clear the marker, but other income deterioration is not re-evaluated on that lane path and is bounded by the original expiry. An affordable saved B with present or committed incoming yard access takes precedence over a feasible lane package rather than being re-ranked against it. Evaluate capacity growth only for B's saved yard/hull and build it when admitted; do not substitute another capacity hull. Keep the marker through B's treasury remittances and regional-funding holds, clearing it only after its build action is durably staged or the build completes, unless expiry or a fresh negative clears it first. A stream invalidation that prevents staging cannot release the savings. The restricted lane handoff never borrows when fresh admission finds B cash-blocked; it holds so the next lane evaluation can decide. Normal capacity savings financing may fund only a freshly evaluated B, never a different cash-blocked candidate.

This is a bounded comparison, not a fleet-income forecast: rates are additive and immediate, with no commissioning lag, shared-depth adjustment between the two new hulls or future nonlinear sprawl correction. The 24-hour history spans fleet changes and can be dominated by one charter. R is remeasured on each evaluation and an absent yard cannot extend the target's expiry. Saving can also outlast a temporary agent at A's yard, so a later build there may require paying to restore access.

### Capacity financing

Capacity growth prices repeatable non-charter business after incumbent commitments and marginal upkeep. Finite charters are excluded entirely from its earning rate, even if their remaining tons and deadline could cover several trips: neither expiry nor a future repost supports a perpetual rate. One-off freight is excluded too. Accepted charter first loads still increase the cargo-lot cash and working buffer. Ordinary voyages and the separate lane-proof path may use finite contracts within their observed limits.

Borrow only for a positive total cash shortfall in an otherwise economically viable capacity build, not merely because cash cannot reach the yard safely. Financing requires repeatable, executable depth-based non-charter earnings; gossip, finite charters and one-off freight cannot support debt. Use Goldsmiths ship liens only: 360 game days, `amortised`, exactly 3,000 annual bps (30% per 360-game-day year), always with recourse. Each eligible unpledged owned ship supports one loan, minimum 10,000 pence and maximum the least of 750,000 pence (7,500 Cr), 40% of its quoted rebuild value and available house funds. No player-loan request is posted.

Before any loan-quote GET, select one economically viable cash-blocked candidate. With no affordable build, rank candidates by depth-backed net return on capital; when the bounded savings comparison beats an affordable build, finance its selected B instead. Perform a cheap mathematical precheck. Include existing obligations, commissioning, working capital and observed remittance fees in the shortfall. Estimate each ten-game-day installment with the 36-period annuity formula `I = P × r / (1 - (1 + r)^-36)`, where `r = 0.30 × 10 / 360`. Principal must cover the positive shortfall plus two estimated installments and fit within `750,000 × eligible unpledged ships`; ten game days at half expected marginal daily net must cover the estimated summed installment by at least 1.25x. Reject before quoting if these conditions fail.

For that candidate, request one exact quote per required collateral ship. If quoted installments change the required principal, requote once at the shortfall plus two quoted installments; accept an exact match or at most two pence of excess per lien. Never bisect or repeatedly requote. The returned terms, collateral cap and house funds are authoritative; shared house funds are not additive across ships. Every loan must meet its minimum and cap, and `0.5 × marginal daily net × 10 / summed installment >= 1.25`. Net after daily interest must remain positive, using each quote's actual annual rate: `principal × annual_rate_bps / 10000 / 360`. Exact financing refusals and canceled financing persist a six-real-hour `_capacity_financing_backoffs` entry keyed by yard and hull, consulted only by financing. Shipyard quote and build refusals keep `_growth_build_backoffs`. A financing refusal or installment-rounding mismatch must not block cash-funded evaluation or erase an otherwise winning savings target.

Freeze admitted capital, working buffer, debt reserve and earnings in the durable financing intent. Protect only this package's incremental cash and planned donor outflows in addition to other missions' reservations. Subsequent draws and construction check authority, the live yard-price ceiling, funded capital and debt cash, and frozen-earnings coverage including interest. At build time, the working buffer may move across the fleet's regions, with at most two pence per lien of tolerance; hard obligations and capital receive no tolerance. Do not rerun the fleet planner or cash-only admission. Temporary stale inputs, unrelated pending work and HTTP 429 hold the phase and honor cooldowns. Borrowed cash may remain idle for at most one game day from the first acceptance; persist that real-UTC deadline even across reconciliation. Expiry, failed economics, a definitive yard refusal, HTTP 403/404 or lost yard presence cancels the package and repays unused debt.

A fresh complete borrower-loan snapshot is mandatory for borrowing and loan mutations. Failed authority retains last-good regional debt reserves, not a whole-balance spending freeze: a loaded ship may still buy safety stores above those reserves, sell, deliver or dock. Missing, stale or incomplete data is never a zero-debt observation. Each active amortised loan protects `max(2 × installment_pence, next_payment_pence, arrears_pence)` in its banking region; defaulted debt and loans without a usable amortised installment protect all owed cash.

Scheduled installments are collected automatically every ten game days. Cure active arrears ahead of discretionary work, including while unrelated actions or treasury work are pending. Fund only the loan region to `max(0, hard reserves - this loan's reserve) + arrears`, then send the cure without waiting for two future installments. Donors retain hard obligations (base reserves, berth bills, freight obligations and other debt), not ordinary cargo reservations. Do not manually retry defaulted claims; the server collects them automatically. Payment, grace and maturity timestamps are real UTC and must not be accelerated again.

Loan acceptance and repayment use immutable journal actions. An HTTP 200 receipt matching the owned loan, quote and collateral identity is authoritative even if its terms changed: record the debt, then cancel and repay an inadmissible package instead of wedging reconciliation. A matching named-loan HTTP 200 repayment is authoritative even if automatic collection changed its counters. Full early repayment omits the amount so the server clears everything owed; fund the observed owed amount plus one game day of interest. Lost-response full repayment needs a zero-owed terminal loan; a lost partial repayment can reconcile from a named-loan repayment-counter increase at least as large as the requested amount. A balance increase alone is never a loan receipt. Borrowed principal is not income; principal repayment is financing cash outflow, while interest and transfer fees remain separate costs.

`capacity_financing:<port>|<hull>` records borrow/no-borrow decisions with a reason and numeric `shortfall_pence`, `lien_capacity_pence`, `principal_pence`, `installment_pence`, `debt_reserve_pence`, `half_net_installment_coverage` and `net_after_daily_interest_pence`. Unknown lien capacity, installment or coverage is `null`, not zero; refusal before exact terms are available is not a measured zero-debt-service case.

## Underwriting and other income

Underwriting is an executable strategy, not a display-only market. It runs after shipping, existing obligations and viable fleet investment have protected their capital. Admission currently uses a minimum 500-basis-point slice of an unchanged **hull-only** policy, excludes every company owned by the player, and requires fresh policy, ownership, cash, catalog, rate and authoritative clock observations. Missing coverage is not zero coverage. Cargo-inclusive policies remain unpriced because a hull sinking outcome does not establish their cargo-loss economics.

At least five valid closed hull-only policies on the same directed lane are required. Duplicate IDs do not increase the sample; changed terms, contradictory terminal outcomes and later nonterminal observations are censored, not counted as safe returns. A Beta(1,1) estimate uses `(losses + 1) / (closed policies + 2)`. Premium must exceed modeled claims and the opportunity cost of currently observed shipping. A fixed-look nominal 95% Wilson upper sensitivity is disclosed separately: neither estimator is a calibrated promise about hull, season, correlation or future traffic. Creation-to-last-update duration is a lock-time proxy, not a settlement deadline.

This cohort pools all recorded eras. It is **not calibrated to the announced cargo-value targeting and ship-capture rules**. The published contract has no reliable deployment boundary or historical cargo, gun, convoy and zone observations with which to reconstruct a comparable post-patch cohort. A hull-only declaration does not prove that a foreign ship is empty or that its whole cargo was declared. The positive-net decision therefore remains a bounded model-based pilot, not measured post-patch expected profit; it must not justify increasing the slice or the permanent loss allowance.

Reserve the full possible collateral loss. The permanent pilot starts at 10% of uncommitted cash; active collateral and realized losses consume it, and only 10% of positive settled underwriting net replenishes it. It does not reset on restart. Before the debit region is known, the slice must fit protected cash in the home region, policy-origin region and every other capable vault. After the first accepted slice, further underwriting waits for a unique, identity-linked collateral debit to an owned vault. An equal-sized unrelated transfer is not proof. Actual vault cash already excludes escrow; do not deduct accepted collateral from that cash a second time.

The immutable 200 response must identify one exact owned slice with present, bounded collateral and premium amounts. Membership or aggregate coverage cannot reconstruct a lost receipt. Preserve ambiguous requests under their original key while unrelated safe fleet work continues. Premium remains an entitlement until an unchanged terminal hull-policy observation establishes earned premium and claims. Safe closure returns principal; loss closure consumes collateral. Net is earned premium minus claims and fees, never collateral returned plus premium. No undocumented insurance fee or voluntary release endpoint is assumed.

| Mechanic | Current economic treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Spot, charters and freight | Executable depth and deadlines; compare compatible loads and destination contribution |
| Source inspection | Explicitly bounded information cost, followed by fresh local pricing |
| Shipbuilding and refit | Supported marginal throughput, complete commissioning cash and observed payback |
| Underwriting | Minimum evidence-positive hull-only pilot with full-loss and shared-capital limits |
| Regional remittance | Observed fee and reserve-preserving funding, not income |
| Capacity financing | Scoped Goldsmiths funding under [Capacity financing](#capacity-financing) |
| Warehouses and factors | Access and storage have recurring/capital costs; ownership is not proof of a profitable trade |
| Futures and market-making | One permanent covered pilot pair only; no speculative income forecast or scale-up |
| Marques | Off by default (`MARQUES_ENABLED`); when enabled, follow the bounded pilot below |
| Convoys | Require economic justification and an owner-provided naming theme before creation |
| Ship disposal and purchase | Players may list and buy ships in port; not used yet because a bought hull keeps its seller's name |

### Pilot limits and underworld observations

The [marque controller](../internal/engine/marque.go) issues at most one active marque, at 500 bps for 60 game hours. Lifetime expired cost minus prizes plus new cost must not exceed 100,000 pence (1,000 Cr). Three expired marques that each earned less prize than their own cost permanently stop the pilot. Count losses over its lifetime, not just consecutive losses. A fresh pre-send quote cannot cost more than the admitted quote and must pass every rule again; the game still has no atomic maximum-price field.

A price increase proven to have prevented sending retires the frozen admission and persists a thirty-real-minute retry delay, matching the cached quote lifetime. Ship work continues during that delay. Any later purchase needs a fresh quote and all normal admission checks; a pending or ambiguous issue request is never retired by this rule.

Never sponsor a zone used by our ships, persisted routes, occupied ports, home port or an underwritten voyage. Missing exposure authority fails closed. Active and pending marque zones constrain new voyages. Existing paid cargo is not stranded merely to reapply this discretionary admission rule. Estimated prize is not earned income. `_marque_outcomes` retains expired results, `_marque_active` retains active exposure, and `_marque_stopped` retains the permanent stop.

The [futures controller](../internal/engine/futures.go) is off by default (`FUTURES_PILOT_ENABLED`). Its single permanent cohort is Amsterdam `water_beer`: one covered 1 t ask and one covered 1 t bid, each with 30-game-hour maturity. The ask strike is 85% and bid strike 115% of the observed NPC top-book midpoint, rounded down in pence. This midpoint is a labelled proxy, not a published settlement formula. These deliberately attractive counterparty prices test mechanics; they do not promise positive carry. The pilot uses a 2 t warehouse lease, unlocked seller stock, buyer space, full buyer strike cash plus margin and customs sensitivity. Exposure is capped at 30,000 pence (300 Cr), including procurement replay; setup and procurement must retain that same spare allowance at staging and dispatch. It does not accept outside listings, redirect ships, hire a factor, replace expired listings, create another cohort or scale up after a win. Missing contract evidence stays unresolved. Complete physical receipts and signed contract cash, plus actual procurement and liquidation receipts, establish realized cohort P&L only after the stock cycle closes; single-leg cohorts use the same rule. Unpriced opening stock or an inventory-only trade reconciliation leaves cash basis unknown. Rent remains explicitly unknown; no tariff or uncollected claim is invented as measured cash. Margin returns and sale proceeds alone are not profit. Transient first-send clock, market or funding failures retain admission terms, yield to ordinary account work and wait through a persisted retry cooldown. Setup lease `403 no_port_presence` pauses rather than ends the pilot and permits another bounded attempt after ordinary presence returns. Immutable-term or cap violations, including increased customs exceeding an immutable bid reserve, end that admission. Turning the flag off stops admissions, not settlement or owned-stock/lease wind-down; expired or disabled admissions release coverage only with positive no-send proof. A lease increased externally above 2 t holds the pilot for operator reconciliation. A default winds down after its physical, penalty and claim facts are known. Its liability clears only when validated post-default own credit shows zero open futures claims; a matching penalty debit leaves the unpaid claim protected. Last validated positive own claims stay reserved until a later valid reduction, even if the capture ages or subsequent reads fail. Aggregate credit replaces older per-contract estimates, while newer defaults retain conservative unattributed coverage. Own credit is read only for unresolved defaults and at most once per five real minutes across restarts, separately from rival budgets. Receipts, ordinary cash movements and fresh vault balances alone do not identify the defaulter.

The [underworld observer](../internal/engine/underworld.go) is observe-only. `underworld-key` creates a separate encrypted company key with exactly `read` and `underworld` scopes. The scope can authorize game mutations, but the observer interface exposes only single-attempt GETs. Each stationary port visit gets at most one wants read; a cove visit also gets at most four contraband-book reads. Reservations survive failures and restart. Missing credentials, HTTP 403 with code `missing_scope`, or observer storage/payload failures disable the observer for that process without stopping trading. Other 403s skip only that read, not the whole observer. Ordinary trade never buys or runs contraband. No smuggling income or unseen buyers enter the planner.

### Game 0.4.0: research, patents and permanent commitments

Sources: [0.4.0 release notes](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/update), [official guide](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) and [OpenAPI](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/api-docs/openapi.json), inspected for the 2026-10-26 release. `GET /update` returns notes newest first; `?since=<version>` limits the history. When the owner says "fetch the update", read this endpoint, the guide and the schema rather than asking for pasted notes. One process-wide, unauthenticated watcher reads the configured API's `/update` at startup and every 15 real minutes without blocking trading. The first successful feed establishes a baseline, including an empty feed; the first later release warns. Restarts establish a new informational baseline. A feed missing the last-seen release is ignored without advancing the cursor. Each poll emits at most five release warnings with bounded fields and fenced-code-aware heading extraction. Jittered exponential failure retries start within one minute and cap at one hour; the isolated public client's limiter feedback does not affect authenticated clients, and its cooldown caps at one hour.

The bot reads only authenticated `GET /v1/rnd/status` and `GET /v1/rnd/register` per account at most every 30 real minutes, including after failures; it does not fetch owned patents, licences or component inventories. Snapshot keys `rnd_status` and `rnd_register` retain the raw responses and dataset metadata; the register is retained only for later rival analysis. Decision kind `rnd` records eligibility, the server's refusal reason, player and entry net worth, the start fee, upkeep per game day and an estimated project cost of `start_fee_pence + 150 × upkeep_per_game_day_pence`. The 150-game-day duration is a planning estimate, not a quoted completion date. Unchanged details deduplicate, but player net worth normally changes, so expect about one `rnd` row per 30 real minutes. Diagnostics apply the generic two-minute stale display between these scheduled polls; no dataset-specific expected-cadence mechanism has been identified. Without an error, this expected stale display is not a fetch failure. No research, patent, licence, manufacturing or fitting investment mutations are automated.

| --- | --- |

## Durable economic record

Keep settled mission economics beyond the seven-day diagnostic window. Acquisition basis, actual proceeds, fees, provisions, and attributable operating costs must survive restarts and pruning. Journal each upstream mutation once and reconcile ambiguous results before recording profit. Transfers, seed capital, borrowed principal, collateral principal, expansion bonuses and unrealized inventory changes are not trading profit. Bonus receipts may explain a net-worth change but never establish repeatable revenue.

Working snapshots retain current requested child datasets and their metadata, not every historically visited market or route origin. A currently required child fetch failure retains its last-good value with explicit stale/error metadata. Durable journals, settlements, and retained diagnostics remain separate from this bounded working state.

Startup logs and skips incomplete registrations, unreadable credential rows, or missing API keys per account. Those failures do not stop healthy account workers or the read-only dashboard API.
