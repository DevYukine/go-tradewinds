This is a partially observed economy. Remote books may be hidden, NPC orders requote, players consume liquidity, and future hazards are uncertain. No strategy can prove global maximum profit from the public information. The objective is the best executable, conservatively valued opportunity among observed candidates, followed by measured reinvestment. Every decision must distinguish an actual quote from an estimate.

## Admiralty commissions and ocean cover (2026-11-02)

The [guide](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md), [OpenAPI](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/api-docs/openapi.json) and [release notes](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/update) were rechecked before this implementation. This section supersedes the historical commission and insurance verdicts below. Neither market is an automatic positive-return assumption.

`__commission` considers fresh Admiralty rows from `GET /v1/contract/list` and matching snapshot `turnkey_cash` quotes, not public Exchange prices. Only unlocked Phase 2 or later yards with a resident factor qualify; no trading ship is pinned to preserve presence. America-region admission waits until the flagship is built whenever it is pending, has an accepted loan or has entered its request retry cycle. The flagship's own build preserves other committed reservations, excluding only its own fixed reserve. That reserve freezes the America balance when pending begins and never exceeds current cash; pre-deploy states without a held amount use the last own flagship request principal instead. Later remittances therefore remain available to their requesting trading or growth intent. Europe remains closed even though the Navy receives the hull. One durable commission runs at a time; the Crown also limits accepted orders across linked companies. Live yard quote requests happen only at first-send preflight, not each idle pass.

Admission funds build plus deposit above existing commitments, using local surplus or one fully covering, reserve-safe remittance. Estimated net deducts build, funding fee, six-game-hour capital carry at 50 bps per game day and deposit deadline exposure of `deposit * 6 / remaining_game_hours`, rounded up. It must strictly exceed 5% of build cost with more than six game hours remaining. Carry and deadline exposure are estimates, not measured debits or calibrated failure probabilities. The controller journals funding if needed, `accept-commission`, then atomic `deliver-commission {contract_id,name,build_mode}`. A name is reserved only after acceptance. Unpaid build cash stays protected from other planners, but a failed pre-acceptance re-evaluation retires the order and books any paid funding fee. Accepted delivery instead needs raw regional cash at least equal to the live quote, and quote no higher than price plus recoverable deposit, because delivery restores the funds atomically. One successful funding remit is allowed before acceptance and one after; never-sent or refused transfers consume no allowance. A remaining shortfall after a successful transfer holds without another fee.

Commission settlements record actual price less actual build cost and fees; returned collateral is principal, not revenue. A pending delivery replays its original journal key for the exact cost receipt. Only after the action replay window closes may a matching terminal builder listing resolve it without a receipt; completed-listing costs then remain explicitly unknown. An authoritative default books the forfeited deposit. Missing builder evidence gets one live active-yard lookup; after expiry plus 15 game minutes, an accepted missing order closes with the deposit loss. Presence refusals receive a short cooldown; other definitive acceptance refusals suppress the order until expiry. A refused, pre-funded order still books its paid remit fee. Ordinary speculative-build backoffs do not block a cash Navy delivery.

Commission P&L stays separate from fleet trading: it supplies no per-hull capacity rate, fleet growth net, cargo-delivery calibration sample or fleet unknown-cost veto. Every closeout first persists an immutable `commission_settle` payload and then inserts it through one settlement-write path. Host observation fixes a terminal fallback's completion boundary; a server `updated_at` is not compared with the host clock. No receivable or loan finances a commission.

Measured market evidence, not our earnings: the seven-game-day Admiralty desk showed two deliveries totaling 267,996.55 Cr at the 2026-11-02 observation. The Boston frigate listing offered 226,032.62 Cr with 22,603.26 Cr collateral; the retained Boston quote was unavailable because material depth was insufficient, so no current executable profit is claimed. The public Havana frigate price was 217,160.85 Cr, but a public price supplies neither a build quote nor local presence. Release 0.7.2 also makes commission supply intermittent and piracy-dependent.

For an ocean-rated hull crossing oceanic water, the departure tail requotes full `max_insurable_pence` after loading and offers the published Lloyd's price only when insured value exceeds free liquid cash after commitments and premium is at most twice published expected loss. A survivable loss is self-insured: rated-hull premiums include a 25-bps loading and are not positive-EV income. Coastal/regional-only hops, missing terms, unknown consorts and no-write quotes buy no cover. Optional cover is skipped when its board/quote is unavailable, protected local cash cannot fund it or the exploration cap would be breached; no premium-only remit is started. Definitive optional refusals cool down for five seconds, not a game day. The published minimum premium is authoritative.

Lloyd's capacity is finite, not a coverage guarantee. A journalled offer waits for full actual cover, or at most six game hours before releasing the ship with its actual partial or zero cover. Freight and charter deadlines can shorten that wait; new cover is skipped when its full wait cannot preserve the existing delivery margin. A persisted policy commits that particular origin/destination leg through ordinary, growth-return and relocation departure paths, so discretionary repricing cannot strand it in port; route safety, stores, freight obligations and policy validity still apply. Each recovery leg retains its own policy identity. Definitively refused offers are not repeated for the same leg, and refits do not change the declaration while waiting. Actual premiums enter mission fees; terminal policy-linked refunds of own escrow reduce fees, while external claims paid into an own vault enter proceeds once. The immutable settlement requires a ledger fetch begun after terminal policy observation. This is capital-risk protection, not claimed positive insurance EV: the published EL is an upper bound, and paying up to twice it can have a negative expected cash return.

The Cape Expedition/world-expansion charter path already admits every fresh open charter, including public expansion rows, without a title or theatre exclusion. The 2026-11-02 expansion board included Port Royal `copper_sheathing` at 1,470 Cr/t and Havana `cannons_arms` at 2,460 Cr/t, marked beyond coastal water. These are posted gross rates, not net forecasts; Crown taper, procurement, customs, travel, provisions and expiry still apply. Existing hard checks reject any under-rated crossing/zone, require executable safe provisions plus port allowance, and require one game day of delivery slack. Regression coverage demonstrates that an ocean-rated, provisionable hull is admitted and a regional hull or missing provisions is refused. No planner bypass or speculative charter-specific fix was necessary.

## Game 0.5.1

The [guide, hazards and golden rules](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) describes variable storm losses. Most storms are light. Rough water and an under-rated hull make heavier losses more likely. A pirate raid that releases the ship takes 20% to 50% of each cargo stack and its stores. Route `safe_provisions_tons` covers the worst storm on any leg, not the worst pirate raid. Keep the hard hull-rating check and buy safe stores.

## Units and clock

All monetary calculations use integer pence: 100 pence = 1 Crown. Freight rewards are per contract; charter rewards are per delivered ton. Rates are basis points. Ship speed is centiknots. The current world clock reports its acceleration; at time scale 60, one game day takes 24 real minutes. Profit rates, operating costs, and payback use game days. HTTP timeouts, diagnostic retention, and the server's 24-hour idempotency window use real time.

Server timestamps, not the workstation clock, govern berth unlocks and contract deadlines. A fresh clock fetch supplies a conservative interval from its request start, completion, and `real_utc`; unlocks use its lower bound and deadline admission uses its upper bound. The acceleration applies when converting elapsed real time to game durations, not a second time to server UTC. HTTP cooldowns and replay windows remain host-time deadlines.

## Comparing opportunities

Admit each shipment on its own destination-ending contribution and time to delivery:

`destination net pence = realizable sale/reward - acquisition - buyer customs - consumed-store replacement - attributable harbour bills and upkeep`

`net pence per game day = destination net pence / delivery duration`

Full safe-store inventory and collateral still have to fit the hold and funded cash budget. Their principal is not all consumed expense. Loading, unloading, berth locks, waiting and loaded sailing speed remain part of delivery duration. Ranking then considers onward work or a priced ballast opportunity cost as described below. This does not commit an empty return. Capacity growth uses its separate repeatable earning forecast after incumbent reservations; a continuation ranking score is not debt-service proof.

Evaluate quantities at actual order-book depth boundaries, available cash, hold capacity, contract demand, and deadline constraints. Buying the full hold can cross into an unprofitable ask level. Selling at the best bid is valid only for the quantity actually supported at that bid. Never treat NPC stock as buy-side demand. Exclude identifiable own-player orders.

Use actual integer customs and fees, conservatively rounding where required. Customs apply to purchases, not to the seller's proceeds. Record actual filled quantity and actual costs from IOC responses; a successful response may still fill zero or only part of the request.

Serial execution and fresh reads between ships prevent the fleet from repeatedly allocating the same cash or liquidity. Unresolved mutations block dependent spending until reconciled.

## Spot, charter, and freight distinctions

### Spot trading

Use complete accessible depth. Gossip has a source time and weaker visibility: it is not a fresh executable book and does not imply an arbitrary volume of demand. Discard unsupported capacity assumptions. A quote becoming visible again does not make an old observation new.

### Event-aware revenue and calibration

The [event pricing policy](../internal/engine/market_events.go) uses temporary world events for each destination and commodity. Structural changes are not temporary reversions. Compare observations and bounded server-time arrivals with both overlapping shocks and intervening transitions. A pre-transition quote crossing a `supply_boom` or another potentially receipt-lowering shock must survive a further 25% revenue stress, even if it predates the start by seconds. Use the later of the current server upper bound and arrival to detect that transition, never host time. Known bid-supporting `demand_shortage` and `admiralty_mobilization` shocks keep the safe aged pre-event fallback. If bids were observed within a shock, unknown arrival or arrival after expiry cannot use those shock bids: use eligible retained pre-event evidence, still subject to the adverse-transition rule, or reject that revenue basis. In-window shock bids also require the 25% stress. Missing or stale event authority applies the same stress to recent observations within 30 real minutes. These are controller risk rules, not measured loss probabilities. Pre-event bids retain commodity-local server observation times, shock-overlapping samples do not teach ordinary bid drift, and other inbound lots consume fresh as well as remembered destination depth.

The [revenue calibration](../internal/store/revenue_calibration.go) fits gross receipts against known-cost, known-proceeds full-lot settlements, not profit ratios. Voyage creation freezes the uncalibrated revenue, basis, exact forecast horizon, quantity and event-stress flag in encrypted mission context; settlement copies that admission forecast without replacing it with a later reprice. Online fitting reads settlements in one query, without replay-plan joins. Offline accuracy may recover legacy admission quotes from the earliest authenticated replay capture; incompatible or already-sailing captures are excluded, and absent captures use the durable quote and recorded duration. `_revenue_calibration_since` stays unchanged at cutover. The separate `_revenue_calibration_v2_since` excludes only retained-basis starts before the raw-gross boundary, preserving unchanged depth, gossip and contract history. Fits cache for one real minute within matching version and boundaries. Online reads at most the latest 4,096 commercial completions within 14 real days, strictly before decision time; shared eligibility excludes shortfalls, partial lots without a matching raw quote, mixed cargo/freight and event-stress receipts. Every child cell needs 30 receipts before overriding its parent; only the market-global and fixed-contract roots need ten. Prefer basis/commodity and basis/group before pooled commodity, group/horizon, group, global/horizon and global. Horizon buckets are `<1`, `[1,1.5)`, `[1.5,2.5)` and `2.5+` game days, including any first-leg delay. Fixed contracts never inherit spot-price misses. A fit error logs a warning and retains the last model or neutral prior, never stopping the runner.

The fit retains receipt ratios up to 20,000 bps, including upside, so symmetric forecast misses do not bias its percentiles downward. Only the applied median is capped at 10,000 bps; calibration never inflates executable forecast revenue. The median receipt factor adjusts expected cargo revenue without the old 90% lower clamp. The 65th percentile adverse revenue miss supplies admission downside, with no double charge of the median adjustment. Unmeasured receipts stay at face value: a pessimistic fixed prior must not block every lot before any evidence can settle. This neutral prior does not remove measured downside, event stress, executable-quote floors or the minimum margin. Fixed freight rewards are excluded from cargo-price haircuts. Plans publish cell, sample count, horizon and adverse haircut in `revenue_calibration_reason`, median factor in `revenue_calibration_bps`, retained add-on in `revenue_age_addon_bps`, quote horizon in `revenue_horizon_game_days` and incremental downside in `price_uncertainty_stress_pence`. Every revenue basis rejects if expected net after price uncertainty is zero or negative, or expected net is below 2% of purchase plus buyer customs. Player-bid gossip must also survive the stressed NPC floor in case the resting player bid disappears; its one-ton fallback keeps the same 25% quote stress. The larger fitted or 25% event downside wins. Diagnostic zone-rating stress remains separate.

The [two-leg ranker](../internal/engine/competition.go) forecasts onward work only for the top three admitted first legs with fresh destination quay depth, sharing one forecast per quay without recursion. Its bounded onward shortlist compares cargo and freight after voyage costs; only cost-dominated one-ton rejections remain eligible for larger-lot sizing. It preserves observation age and applies the first-leg delay; it does not invent a fresh future book. Admission-only growth and hazard lookups skip onward forecasts. If no supported onward trade exists, a priced ballast leg shares its return time, provisions and harbour-cost calculation with the repeat forecast. Rank by `(first net + onward net) / (first days + onward days)` when available, otherwise use the first-leg rate. This is an estimate, not a promised voyage. Competition breaks close economic choices even when every combined score is nonpositive. Computed continuation-rank authority survives plan persistence, and funding fees refresh both single-leg and combined scores without marking standalone plans as continuation-ranked. Capacity therefore retains their separately costed return voyage. Departure and later purchases still require fresh admission. Replay frames retain the same calibration and active-marque inputs as live planning.

### Retained quay books

The [guide, visibility tiers](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) limits live depth to quay presence. The controller therefore keeps our last complete quay bids after departure. These are aged estimates, not current access or guaranteed demand. [Retention and pricing rules](../internal/engine/book_memory.go) keep at most 10 bid levels per commodity for 12 game days and remove our own orders. Failed captures keep the last good entry.

Fresh full depth wins, then sufficiently recent player-bid gossip with enough quantity, then retained bids, then the one-ton gossip probe. Other unsettled inbound lots consume retained bids first. Fresher lower bids cap remembered prices. Retained quotes preserve raw lot revenue for calibration instead of imposing a second `2h+300` stress floor or a 6,000-bps cap. When the fit pools other bases, additional age miss is `ceil(max(0, age_game_days - 1) * drift_bps_per_day)`. A retained-specific cell already measures raw-to-realized age decay and receives no second age charge. Pre-event books age until arrival when a temporary shock expires before arrival, including paid-cargo recovery comparisons. Drift uses the commodity group's adverse p75 after 30 valid observations, then global evidence, then a 100-bps-per-day prior. Samples need at least 0.5 game days between captures, exclude our own fills and shock overlaps, and retain at most 200 entries. The `retained` basis remains an estimate, not depth-backed debt income.

The neutral unmeasured receipt prior never becomes a hidden liquidation discount in a stored sell limit. Measured retained downside and pooled age drift still apply to paid-cargo comparisons. Fresher lower-price caps can overlap measured decay conservatively. Fixed contract receipts share a pool, so face-value freight can dilute charter reward misses. These are model limitations, not measured profit improvements.

### Observed competition

The rolling 24-real-hour competition summary is cached for five real minutes. Cache misses use recorded events, a small owner-name projection and the runner's in-memory snapshot, without display credential decryption or extra game requests. Counts are distinct observed foreign company/ship-name identities in a destination or yard's banking region, not a census or live ship locations. Partial observations, including an observed zero, never establish an empty region. The runner persists continuous fresh quay-observation spans; a region watched for less than the full 24-hour identity window, or with a polling gap over two minutes, stays neutral. Such short coverage does not set either end of the observed range. Missing or short coverage uses the midpoint of eligible regional ship counts, not zero; with no eligible observed spread, competition has no ranking effect. A failed load clears the signal, reports coverage as unavailable and emits at most one warning per five-minute cache interval while economic planning continues.

Among accepted voyages whose ranking score is at least 95% of the economic best, prefer fewer observed foreign ships. The tolerance is anchored to the best candidate, not chained through pairwise comparisons. All safety, funding and profitability decisions remain unchanged. Capacity builds retain the competition evidence for their best repeatable earning lane, not merely their build yard; equal earning rates prefer lower initial store capital first, using competition only if stores are equal, so competition cannot change admission. Funded capacity candidates within 5% of the best net return on hull, gun and store capital prefer the lower-competition earning region. Cash-blocked financing candidates apply the same comparison using depth-backed earnings and their own earning-region evidence. The separate proven-lane growth path uses competition only at equal capital returns and equal daily net.

Expansion yard/partner pairs rank positive historical two-way per-unit spreads from gossip no older than 30 game days. Pairs within 5% of the best score prefer lower observed regional competition. This ranks paid observation, not an executable voyage. World phase is an unlock boundary, not evidence of fewer traders. Never reject a voyage or build solely because another company has many ships. Comparable or selected candidate details include region, coverage, observed companies/ships and the neutral ranking value; unknown counts remain null. Rolling competition details are excluded from decision fingerprints, so changing traffic alone does not defeat diagnostic deduplication.

### Expansion and private charters

Charter delivery occurs at the contract's `origin_port_id`. Only goods carried in from a different port qualify. A local purchase cannot be immediately converted into a charter reward. Track evidenced voyages and eligible quantities; unknown imported history is not proof of eligibility. Exclude stores and sealed freight. Cap delivery by current remaining demand and compare the charter payout with actual executable market proceeds.

Public charters are first-come demand. Check their remaining quantity and expiry before purchase and again before delivery. If demand closes en route, evaluate the actual alternate market rather than pretending the original reward is guaranteed.

### Freight

Freight cargo is sealed and its collateral is tied-up capital, not an expense unless lost. Preserve each accepted obligation, including returned delivery deadline, carrier, destination, quantity, reward, and collateral. Do not count a collateral refund as income. Do not sell, consume, unload, or charter-deliver sealed cargo.

Return freight is evaluated through the ordinary freight planner with collateral, deadlines, hull hazards, safe stores and voyage costs. A contract title does not change admission or create a diagnostic-only stress exemption.

The acceptance deadline is authoritative; listing expiry is not the delivery deadline. Check active and terminal contracts explicitly because listing defaults may show only open offers. A refused delivery can still record a tender. Preserve that obligation and its waiting state instead of treating every 409 as a harmless no-op. Include waiting provisions in the budget.

Compare cargo and compatible freight together before rejecting either component: shared sailing and harbour costs can make the combination profitable even when one alone cannot carry the trip. Recheck remaining quantities and seal occupancy; never allocate the same capacity or cash twice. After a purchase has filled, compare future departure cash with actual executable local recovery. Paid acquisition is sunk for that choice but remains in the final realized profit/loss record.

## Sailing and idle costs

Reject any route whose edge or traversed zone exceeds the ship's hull rating. Check every leg, not just the destination. An attractive silver margin is irrelevant if the route is not legal for the hull.

Use route `safe_provisions_tons`, not the optimistic expected requirement, plus the appropriate port waiting allowance. Crew consume provisions while berthed too. Re-read stores before departure; a partial provisioning fill is not permission to sail. Guns consume hold space and newly built ships do not arrive ready to sail.

Berth handling imposes a minimum 30-game-minute lock, with extra handling time per ton. A departure refusal after a successful purchase must schedule a fresh departure attempt after unlock, never repeat the purchase. Port dues have a minimum six-game-hour berth charge. Anchored roadstead can avoid berth costs but does not erase provisioning needs.

Idle replenishment owns a durable maintenance intent. Roadstead observations may hide executable provisions asks, so one dock can be necessary to inspect the quay. A missing or unaffordable quote then retains the intent instead of repeatedly docking and going offshore. The wait is not free: berth dues and crew upkeep remain protected obligations.

At the quay, the controller quotes the whole residual store requirement at current depth, including buyer customs and conservative integer rounding. If local cash is insufficient, measured regional fees and donor surpluses must cover the complete requirement without consuming any incumbent commitment or operating reserve. Only one immutable remittance is staged at a time; every receipt is followed by fresh pricing and funding checks. Invalidating a never-sent order does not release its still-needed funded cash.

Standalone idle replenishment can use stock claimed only by another unsubmitted idle quote; otherwise shrinking depth can make both ships wait forever. Serialized execution persists the winner's request before another consumer runs. Every attached request keeps its depth claim, other economic consumers still reserve maintenance depth, and all cash commitments remain protected. Held maintenance is reusable fleet capacity, not justification to buy a replacement.

Partial fills, refusals and lost docking receipts resume maintenance; confirmed sufficient stores retire it rather than wait for optional guns. Gun-only docking checks refit affordability and hold capacity first. Mandatory commissioning armament is unchanged. Actual transfer fees and store purchases are expenses, never the transferred principal.

Sound-toll routes are always rejected until an authoritative toll-cost observation source exists. The [guide, marine insurance](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) publishes Lloyd's expected-claim upper bound through `GET /v1/insurance/quote`. Ordinary new voyages charge that bound against purchase cost, buyer customs and freight collateral at risk. It is not a measured probability. An uncalibrated zone storm rating is not a substitute. New-region capacity growth has the bounded, labelled estimate described below. Diagnostic stress remains separate from this expected-net charge.

The [hazard quote controller](../internal/engine/hazard.go) ranks fetches by candidate economics, with deferred pairs ahead on the next pass and primary quotes ahead of laden refinements. It sends at most 30 quote GETs per refresh. Direct hull quotes last 6 game hours; laden lane quotes last 30 game days. Changes in cannons, calendar month or route-zone net piracy invalidate normal reuse. Pricing takes the higher applicable bound. An owned-hull quote on the same lane can serve as a labelled proxy only when its cannon count and hull-category rank are no greater than the priced ship's. Use the highest eligible bound. The quote's piracy observations must cover current route-zone piracy. After a failed refresh, a published eligible lane bound can remain a labelled stale proxy for up to 90 game days, but only within the same winter or nonwinter storm season. Winter is November through February. An ordinary owned-ship voyage without a usable quote or proxy stays unpriced and is rejected, never assigned zero risk.

Missing capacity-growth hazard evidence is cached as a rejection for six game hours in `_growth_hazard_backoffs`, separately from a yard's six-real-hour build-refusal backoff. Repeated candidate, financing and build checks reuse that refusal without repeating decision diagnostics. An active hazard retry is incomplete evidence, not a measured negative or exhausted overseas yard; it must not release European builds. At expiry the candidate is evaluated with current authority again; a cached refusal never authorizes a build or turns an unavailable or stale quote into zero risk. Live read-only decisions on 2026-11-02 confirmed Boston hoy rejections every roughly 30 to 120 seconds before this change.

### New-region zone hazard estimate

For capacity growth only, a region with no live owned ship can use a hazard source beginning `zone estimate` after usable direct quotes and lane proxies have been tried. The label includes `k`, `n` and `Z`. This is a calibrated estimate, not a published Lloyd's bound or a measured loss probability. It does not authorize ordinary owned-ship voyages without their quote evidence.

For each sampled route, `Z_q` sums arrival-zone `base_storm_bps + net_piracy_bps` over every leg. Port arrivals resolve to their catalog sea zone and repeated arrivals count again. Samples use the quote's observed piracy. A target's `Z` uses current piracy with the same arrival-node calculation. Missing zone or piracy observations, negative inputs, an empty route or a nonpositive sampled `Z_q` leave calibration unavailable.

Samples must be laden quotes from currently owned, non-sunk, non-convoy ships, with no error or illegal result, a known route, nonnegative expected loss, hull-category rank no higher than the target and no more cannons than the target. Quotes must not be future-dated and must be no older than 90 game days in the same winter or nonwinter storm season. For the growth target, cannons are `min(4, hull.max_cannons)`. Require `n >= 10`; `k` is the nearest-rank p90 of `expected_loss_bps / Z_q`. Then `E = min(10000, ceil(k * Z))` bps. Each target leg must be at most 2 times the longest sampled leg, and its arrival-zone storm-plus-piracy risk must be at most 1.5 times the highest sampled leg risk. Hull rating, Sound-toll rejection, safe stores, executable prices, normal profitability and payback still apply.

An estimated-hazard build also needs the latest positive, finite `net_worth_pence` metric, published no more than 6 real hours ago and not in the future. Exposure is quoted hull price plus the 10% full-quote slippage reserve, cannons and initial stores, gross of mortgage principal. It must be at most 35% of net worth, using integer pence: exactly 35% accepts and one penny more rejects. Cash, mortgage and financed build preflights recheck this limit and valuation freshness. Missing or stale valuation makes the estimate unavailable; it is not a zero valuation or a claim that the lane loses money.

## Treasury and reinvestment

Cash is regional. Escrow is not spendable. Reserve enforcement includes outstanding obligations, collateral, near-term bills, and working capital in the billing region. Any negative vault, or any underfunded active/obligated region, blocks discretionary construction while safe income voyages continue. Empty unused regions need no artificial reserve. A remittance pays an observed fee and must preserve its source's protected funds. Several donors can fund a shortfall through sequential partial transfers; each durable transfer is followed by fresh balances before selecting the next donor.

Regional funding matches the current shortfall without working-capital buffers, spend forecasts, minimum remittance sizes or donor/recipient cooldowns. Tiny exact remits are acceptable; fee rounding must still cover the shortfall. Relocation does not block company bill funding. Each donor still preserves protected obligations and other missions' unpaid commitments.

Crown rates conservatively estimate unsent transfers. Since [0.7.0 bank drafts](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/update), a cheaper bank may carry the remit automatically. Record only the receipt's validated `fee_pence`, not `crown_fee_pence`, as the paid expense; transfer principal remains cash. The audited ping-pong window contained 23 remits and about 193 Cr of fees in 2.4 real hours. At time scale 60, that is about 32 Cr per game day if sustained, not the earlier research estimate of 965 Cr. The avoidable share and post-change saving remain unmeasured.

The triggering mission owns each admitted transfer and records its validated actual fee once through the existing expense path. A remittance that partly funds a shortfall does not pin an allowance for a future batch; the next transfer uses fresh balances and current requirements. Optimizing the measured small fee leak must not idle a profitable hull.

Ordinary positive-contribution voyages can draw on reserve-safe donor cash instead of being limited to their origin vault. Quote the full immutable cargo-order bound, customs, safe-store cash, collateral, fleet bills and destination funding together; pure freight does not invent an ordinary-cargo purchase. Include observed remittance fees once in destination net, stress and repeat forecasts. The ship mission owns each sequential transfer, so company treasury work cannot pre-fund a merely conditional destination. Revalidate the pinned opportunity before funding or procurement and again at first send after rate-limit waits. If it disappears before cargo is acquired, settle paid fees as an aborted voyage rather than deleting them.

Once spot cargo is paid for, the ship compares sailing on against selling it locally. A destination quay book goes stale as soon as no ship remains there to refresh it, which is usually right after buying for it. Holding paid cargo in port until new evidence appears only accrues dues. For ordinary non-event cargo without a fresh destination book, the tons aboard are valued at the per-ton sell floor accepted at purchase (worst depth fill, stressed NPC bid or one-ton probe floor; never freight income), lowered by any fresh gossip floor. A fresh but thin book counts only the receipts that fill now. A fully executable local exit that beats this value after bills still liquidates locally. This valuation never authorizes a new purchase, and the arrival sale still walks live bids without a price floor.

An admitted event quote is not a permanent sell floor. [Paid-cargo recovery](../internal/engine/procurement.go) compares executable local receipts after conservative extra handling with a usable retained/pre-event destination estimate calibrated for forecast horizon and current age, after the destination stay and consumed stores. Use measured receipt factors for paid retained cargo; the unmeasured 10% admission prior protects new spending, not forced liquidation of paid cargo, so an unmeasured paid estimate retains age drift alone. Local recovery at or above estimated net selects liquidation even below acquisition cost: acquisition is sunk and stays in realized profit/loss, not the comparison threshold. Only without a usable destination estimate does known acquisition cost supply a conservative fallback floor. Below that floor, with partial local liquidity, or with neither estimate nor known acquisition cost, continue to the original destination under existing route, stores and freight safety gates.

Stationary harbour obligations follow actual presence; sailing obligations follow the final authoritative route leg, not the departed origin retained in a ship record. A prospective destination still needs the engine-owned 1,000 Cr regional floor and the new arrival bill even when no current hull obligates that region. Only the home region adds three game days of fleet and factor upkeep, also protected by the company's automatic bill-remittance setting. These are liquidity protections, not additional voyage expenses.

The guide's daily fleet upkeep includes a nonlinear coastal/regional component:

`45 Cr × (max(0, coastal/regional tons + floor(oceanic tons / 2) - 40) / 100)^1.45`

It also includes `120 Cr × factors^1.25` and `1 Cr × oceanic ton`. The [guide, treasury and daily bills](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) gives these terms. [DailyUpkeep](../internal/engine/planner.go) rounds the combined nonlinear charge upward in pence. Use the marginal change from adding a hull or factor, not a standalone or fleet-average cost.

The cash order is operating obligations and debt service first, then any loan refinance in flight, then admissible cheap-hull investment or its bounded savings target from free cash above the unsecured-loan payoff hold, then surplus prepayment. While the hold binds, savings targets are cleared. Prepayment is also tried when an overseas growth hold ends the growth pass without staging anything. [Prepayment](../internal/engine/loans.go) requires fresh debt and cash authority, no growth intent or live savings target, no open refinance request or refinance waiting to repay, and all regional reserves funded. A capacity hold for an unrelated reason does not block it. In the loan's region, keep those reserves plus `(non-sunk fleet size + 1) × cargo-cash lot`. The repaid loan's own installment reserve is released by the payoff, so it does not count against the floor. The payment budgets the observed balance plus one game day of interest and omits the amount, so the server settles the exact balance at execution. Only pay an active owned non-mortgage loan when that budget fits above the floor. First-send checks repeat this protection. It does not remit just to prepay, drain cargo float, or manually prepay mortgages. Scheduled debt service still runs ahead of growth.

While the company owes an unsecured loan, the [debt-service hold](../internal/engine/loans.go) keeps the remaining payoff (balance plus one game day of interest, less the two-installment reserve already held) out of discretionary spending. New hulls, capacity savings targets, factor expansion, futures and new growth borrowing may only use free cash above that hold, and growth reports `growth deferred: free cash must keep the remaining unsecured loan payoff` while it binds. Cargo buying, stores, provisions, refits, berth dues, customs, tolls, commissions, underwriting, marques and arrears cures keep the normal regional operating floor, so the fleet keeps earning while the loan is paid down. Mortgages and ship-lien loans do not create a hold; their payments are already sized into the build that took them.

The game caps loan terms at 360 game days, so a loan cannot be extended in place. When free cash outside other reserves falls below the smaller of three installments and the payoff, the [refinance mission](../internal/engine/loan_refinance.go) posts one unsecured amortised 360 game day request for the payoff rounded up to 1,000 Cr in the loan's region, open 72 game hours. It only posts when a fresh schedule at the same rate would cut the installment to at most 90% of today's, which needs about 31 or fewer installments left; relief grows as the loan amortises. It approves only the lowest-rate quote whose installment is at most 90% of the current one and whose rate is at most twice the Goldsmiths rate, with at least one game hour remaining before both quote and request expire. A refused quote is rejected individually while the request stays open for other quotes. No acceptable quote leaves the request waiting; a lapsed or refused borrower request backs off for ten game days while trading continues. The proceeds stay earmarked until full repayment of the old loan is confirmed by fresh loan data. Unconfirmed or refused payoffs wait for fresh debt evidence and retry with a clock-derived delay, stopping after three failed attempts; successful repayment receipts do not count against that bound. A mismatched disbursement or exhausted repayment retries quarantines the refinance and retires its controller, releasing the earmark and prepayment block so both loans become ordinary debt under the debt-service hold and normal prepayment rules. Arrears cures run before refinancing, and surplus prepayment waits only while its request or payoff is in flight.

New refinancing is blocked while more than one own active or defaulted unsecured non-mortgage loan exists. A defaulted replacement still counts; repaid, recovered and written-off loans do not. An already-approved refinance still repays its old loan without rechecking that eligibility gate. Quarantine or exhausted payoff retries retain a ten-game-day per-old-loan backoff, so closing the replacement does not immediately start another request before that rate limit expires. The backoff survives restart without reserving proceeds or blocking ordinary prepayment. Eligibility and first-send preflight checks use captured state without fetching borrower history.

There is no ship-count cap; recalculate depth, marginal upkeep and cash after every purchase.

Shipyard quotes are exact observations, not binding price locks: the build endpoint has no quote token or maximum-price precondition. Allocate source, destination and contract reservations only to actual competing lanes, including committed sailing returns; unrelated hulls do not consume every lane's liquidity. Repeat throughput uses each committed lot and full cycle duration. Whole outstanding lots are reserved once, not once per future cycle; already delivered ordinary cargo on a returning ship consumes no remaining contract or bid capacity. Preserve unknown cost state rather than manufacturing profit. ### Measured capacity growth

The [capacity controller](../internal/engine/growth_capacity.go) evaluates hoy, cutter, brigantine and collier hulls plus catalog `oceanic` hulls that cannot seal dispatches (including barque). Dispatch hulls remain excluded. This does not add large hulls to ordinary lane growth, factor selection or expansion-gossip loops. Capacity needs accepted repeatable non-charter work after incumbent commitments. Future-dated settlement evidence vetoes every hull.

Once any shipyard in Phase 2 or later is unlocked, Phase 1 yards, including Europe, are permanently closed to new hull construction and new build financing. New hulls must be built in America or another unlocked region with a yard in Phase 2 or later, including Phase 3 regions. Exhausted overseas options, missing evidence, funding waits and retry deadlines never reopen Phase 1. Remove Phase 1 candidates before build, savings and financing selection, including selection restricted to one yard, and preserve the saved overseas yard and hull index after filtering. Clear obsolete Phase 1 savings and growth port markers so they cannot reserve cash or prevent a probe. Lane growth excludes Phase 1 yards before ranking.

Exhaustion controls regional probe scheduling, not permission to build in Europe. A region is exhausted while a confirmed paid exploration's realized loss retry is running; a paid pair's separate unevaluated failure retry is running; a fresh complete yard evaluation found no viable or buildable capacity and its 24 real hour capacity retry is running; every eligible yard and partner fails a hard measured exploration gate, meaning arrival risk above 1.5 times the calibrated maximum or no unlocked hull rated for its water; or no usable yard and partner exists. Regional presence away from a yard does not exhaust the region or prevent an evidence probe. Without yard presence or a live pair, fewer than two free factor slots prevents a new pair. Refusals before any factor is paid record only a retry, not a realized loss. Unexplored regions and ordinary waits for retry deadlines, the 24 real hour paid start floor, funding, existing exploration, freshness or treasury work do not exhaust a region. Phase 1 holds name regional reasons and the earliest known overseas retry; an expired deadline requires overseas consideration again without reopening Europe.

Retire persisted Phase 1 financing only before the first loan is accepted, and only when fresh catalog, clock, ship, factor and zone authority confirms an unlocked overseas yard. Delete local financing intents that were never sent and release their reservations; remote open requests lapse unapproved. Once any loan is accepted, finish the admitted build under its ordinary safety, funding and deadline checks. Overseas exhaustion, retry changes or stale authority must not abandon that accepted package or trigger repayment fees. Preserve possibly sent immutable journal actions for normal reconciliation. Retirement belongs to admission and the financing driver, not a send precondition veto or repeated staging loop.

Cash-funded cheap hulls do not wait for a measured earnings cohort. Their accepted work, marginal upkeep, cash, safe stores and payback checks still apply. Oceanic estimates use the lower of depth-plan rate and measured class rate; a class without settlement history uses the measured fleet rate, with no arbitrary haircut. Every debt package uses the same conservative net: `min(depth-forecast rate, realized settlement net per hull-game-day) - incremental upkeep - existing interest`, also deducting the proposed mortgage's interest. Measure over the trailing 30 game days, clipping each surviving hull's age to that window; at least 24 hull-game-days, known trading costs and a fresh settlement are required. Stale class history cannot borrow a fresher fleet rate. Missing or stale measurement refuses debt with `measured_net`. Retained books, gossip, finite charters and one-off freight cannot support recourse debt. These are controller rules, not game guarantees.

| Build | Rate and payback treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Cheap cash hull | Predicted repeat rate less marginal upkeep; no measured-income wait |
| Cheap mortgage hull | Fresh realized class/fleet net bounds the depth rate; deduct upkeep and interest and verify mortgage coverage |
| Oceanic non-dispatch hull | Lower depth/measured rate, mortgage slot and full package economics; no blanket admission |
| Dispatch or other non-capacity hull | Rejected, including as savings targets |

Payback is committed hull, quote cushion, gun and store capital divided by positive incremental daily net. It must fit the engine-owned 420-game-day capacity horizon. Decisions record predicted, measured and used rates plus sample hull-game-days. These are estimates of future payback, not measured future earnings. See the [capacity calculation](../internal/engine/growth_capacity.go).

### Paired regional exploration

The [expansion controller](../internal/engine/growth_factor.go) buys private observation at an unlocked shipyard in Phase 2 or later and one distinct partner port in the same banking region. This includes eligible Phase 3 yards. Both ports must be unlocked, outside coves and on coastal or regional water; only the yard must have a shipyard and a phase of at least 2. Regional ship or factor presence away from a yard does not block an evidence probe; stationary presence at an unlocked overseas yard instead feeds ordinary capacity evaluation without another pair. Phase 1 home and Baltic yards never receive paid exploration pairs, even when our fleet is elsewhere. Overseas yards must still pass the ordinary capacity safety and economic checks. The permanent Phase 1 ban determines where new hull capital is allocated, not whether unsafe or unprofitable work becomes acceptable.

Rank all unblocked yards using eligible gossip no older than 30 game days. Pair ranking requires an observed outbound spread and scores `max(outbound) + max(0, inbound)` without flooring a negative outbound. For admission, multiply hold after guns and safe stores by each direction's bid-minus-ask spread, then apply a 50% haircut. Subtract origin purchase customs plus 1p per cargo ton, store costs including customs, berth dues and calibrated expected laden hazard loss. Divide by publicly observed round-trip game days, including queues, speed and turnaround. Use the better direction and label the result `gossip estimate`. Complete regional realized earnings with known proceeds and costs, measured per hull-game-day, supersede gossip. Historical prices estimate an information purchase, not executable trading income.

When an unlocked overseas region without yard presence has no positive-spread gossip pair, admit an evidence-only paid probe without a projected build or observed hull quote. Prefer a `yard_discount` yard, then lower current arrival-zone hazard and port ID. Choose a distinct non-cove same-region regional-water partner not already served by a factor, with the most retained gossip bid/ask observations; ties prefer lower arrival hazard, then port ID. No observations are required. Both ports still need measured safe arrival risk, a water-rated unlocked capacity hull, current authority and fresh net worth. Protect both 750 Cr hire fees, measured transfer fees and the region's protected reserve through the existing treasury remittance path. Persist `probe: true` on both reciprocal records so hire preflight rechecks probe safety/funding without asking for a gossip-backed build. Record `overseas probe: no gossip spread, hiring factors for quay evidence`. The fresh quay books and yard quote then feed the existing capacity evaluator; a probe never authorizes a hull build by itself.

For a gossip-backed pair, first try a projected cheap-hull build using normal payback, a matching observed turnkey hull quote, fresh authority, available cash or a valid mortgage, and estimated-hazard calibration. A non-cash rejection, including an unprofitable estimate or missing hull quote, falls back to the region's evidence probe in the same pass: `overseas probe: estimated build rejected, hiring factors for quay evidence`. A purely cash-blocked viable build waits for cash to accumulate, does not buy a probe or release Europe, and records `overseas build waits for <amount> Cr cash shortfall: <capacity reason>`. Paid exploration does not require measured fleet earnings. Estimate records retain their quote source and stale flag. Admission prices nonlinear marginal hull and two-factor upkeep, both 750 Cr fees and measured remittance fees. Working cash covers existing fleet lots plus one overseas candidate lot, not that lot multiplied by fleet size. Gossip-backed pairs and probes require `factors + 2 <= 12`; current treasury work or a live overseas savings target prevents another pair.

Both proposed ports' current storm-plus-piracy arrival risk must fit the calibration's 1.5-times maximum. Net worth must be positive, finite, no more than 6 real hours old and not future-dated. Gross exposure is hull price plus the 10% full-quote cushion, guns and initial stores, without deducting mortgage principal. It must not exceed 35% of fresh net worth. A mortgage still needs current loan authority, no mortgage cooldown and measured earnings for that hull class. Construction still needs a fresh live yard quote and accepted repeatable work at executable prices.

Persist the selected reciprocal `_expansion_agents` records and their funding and exploration intent before the first remittance. Resume that selection without reranking. Existing `__treasury` remittance funds both fees and the regional reserve before hiring. Partner hiring preserves the reserve plus its remaining fee. A recorded partner can lack a shipyard and can finish hiring while the yard becomes a live savings target. First-send guards recheck the applicable admission and funding rules.

Admission and hire first-send use the same projected post-remittance cash: protect the new region's base reserve and both pending hire fees exactly once and charge the observed transfer fees. Gossip-backed pairs additionally test hull capital and working lots against what remains; probes leave that build decision to fresh quay evaluation. Persisting the pair must not make an unchanged funded admission unaffordable.

Keep both factors for at least 2 real hours from the later first-confirmed `HiredAt`. A fresh yard quote, both fresh quay books and an actual route-backed capacity evaluation persist `evaluated_at`. Quote failures are not evaluations. After the floor, retain the pair only for a growth port, live savings target or active mission/plan reference. Settled historical plans do not count. Charge both factors' upkeep to a candidate when they are its sole regional presence. Definitive unused hire rejection releases awaiting records and leaves any surviving member unpaired. Transient preflight failures preserve the durable pair within its awaiting window.

A proven never-sent precondition, terms or quote veto is scoped to its owning mission, not a fleet-wide resynchronization barrier. Persist a 15-game-minute retry unless the domain already has a retry rule, and let other controls and ships continue in the same pass. Routine financial/control epoch invalidation, including restart, replans or restamps on the next pass without adding a cooldown. A transient hire veto persists both members' `retry_at`, capped by the original two-real-hour awaiting expiry. No funding caller may overwrite an existing cooled treasury intent. A package's donor transfer spends its own exact mission-generation reservation once; independent obligations remain protected. Ambiguous or possibly sent requests retain their journal safety barrier.

Try every unblocked ranked yard. A fee-free rejection falls through in the same pass without a global cooldown. Awaiting hires block another exploration only until `pairTime + 2 real hours`; delete unhired records at expiry. If neither factor was hired, retry one real hour after the awaiting window (three real hours after selection), without consuming the paid-start floor. Any unevaluated paid pair, gossip-backed or evidence-only, advances a separate 24/48/96-real-hour backoff once per attempt, not per dismissed factor, and exhausts its region during that retry. A fresh route-backed capacity evaluation resets that counter. A realized fee/economic loss requires at least one confirmed paid factor; an unpaid rejection cannot manufacture exhaustion. `_expansion_agent_outcomes` applies a separate 24/48/96-real-hour economic-loss ladder and stores measured capacity rejection/retry independently. Gossip and quote changes do not reopen paid-failure backoffs. A confirmed or reconciled landed build resets these outcomes. `_expansion_last_started_at` is written on the first confirmed hire, not selection or remittance; both probe and gossip-backed pairs share one paid start per 24 real hours.

A new hire does not add routes to a refresh already in progress. The next refresh requests the pair's books, yard quote and outward and return route geometry. This can leave capacity evaluation waiting one pass for routes. Within the 2 real hour observation window, awaiting hires or a missing fresh capacity evaluation hold new cash builds, new debt and voluntary loan prepayment. Mandatory obligations and ordinary fleet work continue. Ending that window or completing the evaluation ends the pair's safety hold, but Phase 1 construction and new financing stay permanently closed once an overseas yard is unlocked. An unsuccessful exploration admission is a quiet, deduped `expansion_agent:<region>` decision with the actual region and rejected build or probe details. Preselection skips use `unavailable`. An admitted pair records `expansion_agent`.

Once an overseas build wins, ordinary `__treasury` remittance funds its yard region without consuming protected donor or local reserves. No separate overseas transfer path is used. The durable `__growth` control builds and arms the ship, then hands it to normal planning.

### Mortgages

The [guide, ship mortgages](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md), read on 2026-10-28, allows 60% of the charged build price, or 80% for oceanic hulls, over 180 game days with installments every 10 game days. Only one owing mortgage is allowed. The hull has a lien with recourse. The [mortgage rules](../internal/engine/loans.go) require complete available quoted terms. Reserve-safe cash funding wins when remittance fees are no greater than total mortgage interest. A mortgage may cover a shortfall or replace dearer remittance only after fresh realized-net coverage. Deduct `(total repayable - principal) / 180` from daily net. Protect the down payment, 10% full-quote cushion, guns, stores, normal working cash and two installments. Oceanic capacity may combine this mortgage with one loan source, but the researched barque remains subject to every ordinary funding and exposure gate.

Builds are turnkey. Own-material builds are not used.

### Passenger fares

The [guide, passengers](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md) describes whole groups choosing hourly between offers. Berths consume hold. Fares pay on arrival, lateness reduces them, and insurance does not cover them. The [passenger controller](../internal/engine/passengers.go) uses idle ships and one destination per passage, with no fleet-wide cap since 2026-10-31 (see the 0.7.2 verdicts). Its fare floor covers the whole group's modeled passage cost, priced ballast repositioning and Lloyd's bound. Never offer below it.

Buy required provisions before going offshore to post offers. Keep an open discretionary fare for at least six game hours before cancelling it for an accepted cargo quote. Re-ladder only after 12 game hours. After cargo cancellation, ordinary fresh planning selects the voyage; there is no nested cargo handoff. A descending replacement price is at most 85% of the prior price unless the floor binds. Start at the floor when there is no time for a full ladder step and the next offer's six-hour lifetime before `board_by`; never shorten the lifetime to chase a boarding deadline. Cancel and replacement are separate journaled writes. Exclude route zones with net piracy at least 1,000 bps or an active foreign marque. Stress arrival must meet the offered promise before boarding. After boarding, require a matching durable passage and safe stores, not a renewed on-time promise check. Lateness reduces fare; holding aboard groups indefinitely only makes it worse. Count only event-backed paid receipts as income; offer prices remain estimates.

The lifetime and ladder gates apply to discretionary changes, not to closing an unsafe or below-cost offer or departing with already-boarded promises. Recheck route safety and the current whole-group cost floor while an offer is open. Cargo switching must retain any groups that board while the cancellation is in flight.

When accepted lane fares are sparse, seed the ceiling from the matching distance band's median in unauthenticated `GET /v1/passengers/fares/summary`, while retaining the modeled cost floor. Retain and validate only the bands, not unused world-wide unmet-demand rows. The public median is a pricing anchor, not confirmed lane demand or guaranteed revenue. Do not extrapolate the highest observed fare by 50% on a long, poorly sampled route.

The [receipt reconciler](../internal/engine/passenger_reconcile.go) can release a completed passage when terminal events are missing. It first needs authoritative empty holds and no aboard groups at the destination, sustained for 2 game hours. Proceeds and costs remain unknown, not zero-income evidence. Archived group context lets late paid/lost events upgrade the same open settlement without counting it twice. The [receipt scheduler](../internal/store/passenger_receipts.go) scans at most 32 due rows per batch, with a persisted 15-real-minute retry interval. After seven real days it closes an unresolved receipt with proceeds still unknown. [Transient passenger failures](../internal/engine/passengers.go) hold and retry with the original intent. Only integrity failures quarantine. The [recovery path](../internal/engine/passenger_quarantine.go) archives damaged accounting and recovers the hull from fresh aboard and offer evidence, without recording invented fare income or stopping unrelated fleet work.

The [fare capture gate](../internal/engine/passenger_capture.go) survives failed captures, brief departures and restarts. It expires retained fare evidence only after 10 authoritative game hours of lane irrelevance. An unavailable waiting capture cannot prove irrelevance or grant another fare read.

### Capacity saving

Buying the best affordable hull now is not always the fastest way to grow fleet earnings. Compare that hull A with every economically viable, cash-blocked hull B supported by repeatable executable depth. B must outrank A under the ordinary capacity return-on-capital rule using both its build-time earning net and its depth-backed net, with the same best-anchored 5% competition band for each comparison. A higher absolute income alone is not enough. A yard supported by a recorded expansion pair can become an overseas savings target; a legacy solo expansion agent alone cannot.

Measure the fleet's savings rate R in pence per game day from the last 24 real hours: divide recorded settlement `NetPence + UnknownLossPence` by `window seconds × time_scale / 86400`, then subtract one tenth of each active amortised borrower loan's installment. Settlement expenses already include claimed upkeep bills, so do not deduct fleet-and-factor upkeep again. Recorded charters count toward R, even though they never support B's repeat income. Unknown-cost losses count negatively; routine unknown-cost rows do not switch saving off. The existing future-completion hold still applies. A nonpositive R disables saving.

Let Ca be A's hull price, quote cushion, guns and initial stores; ra its incremental net per game day; Sb B's current total cash shortfall; rb B's depth-backed incremental net; and L one additional cargo-cash lot. Compare both purchase orders:

- `tSave = Sb / R`
- `tBuyThenB = (Sb + Ca + L) / (R + ra)`
- `tSaveThenA = tSave + (Ca + L) / (R + rb)`
- `margin = rb × (tBuyThenB - tSave) - ra × tSaveThenA`

Save only for a strictly positive margin and a positive shortfall, and only when `tSave <= 24 real hours × time_scale / 24` game days, which is 60 game days at scale 60. Choose the B with the largest positive margin. Equality buys A now. Try normal capacity financing for that B only when `(rb + ra) × tSave` exceeds estimated total interest, `36 × installment - principal`; otherwise waiting earns more than the avoided wait costs. A financing refusal retains the cash instead of building A. An affordable A is not deferred to finance a B outside this bounded savings rule. Once B is affordable, prefer it unless A wins the same ranking with an earning net at least as high as B's; equality does not justify paying more capital for the same earnings.

The durable `_capacity_savings_target` records the yard, hull, A-independent required cash, capital, depth net and depth competition preference. Required cash is current available cash plus B's evaluated shortfall, including only fees accounted for by that evaluation. Its real-UTC expiry is the evaluation time plus `min(maxWait, 1.25 × tSave) × 86400 / time_scale` seconds: 25% slack for a noisy savings estimate, still bounded by the measured history window. A fresh winning evaluation may refresh its economics and shorten its expiry, but cannot extend the same yard/hull's deadline. A still-viable cash-blocked B retains its target when no affordable A exists. Fresh unavailability, mismatched quote identity, a nonviable or losing evaluation, or a construction backoff drops it; expiry and a completed build drop it too. Missing or stale quotes/books, request cooldowns and non-quay observations leave the yard unevaluated and do not clear the target. Losing yard presence alone does not release the cash: an affordable capacity hull or lane-proof package must beat the live target by the same window-bounded comparison. Once B is affordable without presence, hold only while a ship's current voyage or committed mission ends at that yard; otherwise release the target rather than wait without a route back.

An expired yard/hull is recorded in `_capacity_savings_expired` and cannot restart a savings hold until another build completes or 24 real hours pass, whichever comes first. This exclusion survives a pass with no affordable A and a process restart; expiration must release the original deadline rather than silently begin another wait for the same hull.

While saving, `_capacity_growth_port` stays empty, new expansion-pair admission waits, and new underwriting collateral and marque purchases wait. Keep both members of a recorded pair when either is the saved yard, even before a build mission exists. An already-recorded partner may finish hiring while the savings marker is live. A viable cash-blocked overseas B remains protected even when no affordable A exists. Existing ships continue earning and servicing obligations. `capacity_savings:<port>|<hull>` records rounded game days and whole-Crown rates and capital, with the hold reason `capacity growth saves for a better hull`.

A feasible lane package compares against B's stored economics while B remains cash-blocked. Fresh negative quote observations still clear the marker, but other income deterioration is not re-evaluated on that lane path and is bounded by the original expiry. An affordable saved B with present or committed incoming yard access takes precedence over a feasible lane package rather than being re-ranked against it. Evaluate capacity growth only for B's saved yard/hull and build it when admitted; do not substitute another capacity hull. Keep the marker through B's treasury remittances and regional-funding holds, clearing it only after its build action is durably staged or the build completes, unless expiry or a fresh negative clears it first. A stream invalidation that prevents staging cannot release the savings. The restricted lane handoff never borrows when fresh admission finds B cash-blocked; it holds so the next lane evaluation can decide. Normal capacity savings financing may fund only a freshly evaluated B, never a different cash-blocked candidate.

This is a bounded comparison, not a fleet-income forecast: rates are additive and immediate, with no commissioning lag, shared-depth adjustment between the two new hulls or future nonlinear sprawl correction. The 24-hour history spans fleet changes and can be dominated by one charter. R is remeasured on each evaluation and an absent yard cannot extend the target's expiry. Saving can also outlast a temporary agent at A's yard, so a later build there may require paying to restore access.

### Capacity financing

Capacity growth prices repeatable non-charter business after incumbent commitments and marginal upkeep. Finite charters are excluded entirely from its earning rate, even if their remaining tons and deadline could cover several trips: neither expiry nor a future repost supports a perpetual rate. One-off freight is excluded too. Accepted charter first loads still increase the cargo-lot cash and working buffer. Ordinary voyages and the separate lane-proof path may use finite contracts within their observed limits.

Borrow only for a positive total cash shortfall in an otherwise economically viable capacity build, not merely because cash cannot reach the yard safely. The incumbent buffer must already be our own cash: `surplus >= WorkingCapital - candidateLotCash`. Working capital is `max(reserve, 15% of hull price) + (FleetSize - DeployedLots) × medianLotCash + candidateLotCash + 2 × mortgageInstallment`. Cargo already aboard or positive mission-reserved cash counts an incumbent lot once, never twice. Borrow for capital, the candidate's lot, two new loan installments and the bounded headroom below, not to repair an incumbent cash deficit. Gross outstanding principal of all existing loans and mortgages plus the new mortgage/loan package must not exceed 35% of positive net worth observed within six real hours; already accepted loans in this package count only once. The separate zone-estimate gross hull/commissioning/lot cap remains.

The first funding source is one own player-loan request in the yard's banking region, usable by the Kraken Cartel or any other non-own lender. Post `amortised`, 360 game days, recourse, `auto_quote:true`, `open_game_hours:24` and `collateral_kind:"ship_lien"` with the selected `ship_id`; never post unsecured. Select the highest estimated rebuild free owned ship using the cheapest observed catalog/yard class price plus 150 Cr per gun, tie-breaking by ship ID; skip unknown prices, pledged or mortgaged ships, open listings and ships named by another open own request. Refuse with `debt_capacity` when no priced free ship exists or principal exceeds that ship's estimated rebuild; the lender decides its own loan-to-value limit. This is an estimated rebuild ranking, not an appraisal quote; the server rechecks collateral at approval. Recourse and the uninsured lien risk remain ours, and the request publicly exposes the server's borrower record.

Before posting, size the smallest integer principal covering the shortfall, one candidate lot and one game day of current fleet upkeep, plus twice the rounded 36-period annuity installment at 3,000 annual bps. That explicit headroom absorbs normal bills and lot transitions during the wait; current own surplus can cover a remaining deficit only within the posting-time own-capital share. The same fresh realized-net bound sizes principal (`P <= 4 × net / annuityFactor`) and checks half-net ten-game-day coverage (`4 × net >= installment`). For a mortgage plus one loan source, also require `10 × net before proposed mortgage interest >= 1.25 × (mortgage installment + loan installment)`. Net after the loan's actual daily interest must stay positive and capital payback must fit the same engine-owned 420-game-day capacity horizon. No request or instant quote is created when these prechecks fail.

The durable `__growth` player-loan wait freezes other growth for the 24-game-hour request window (24 real minutes at time scale 60), protecting only the package's pre-existing capital and debt-reserve share, never anticipated proceeds or incumbent working lots. Recheck the fleet buffer at approval. Refresh own requests and loans on every capture. A valid quote must name the recorded request, exact region/principal/360-day amortised terms and a non-own lender; its rate may not exceed the request's published `goldsmiths_rate_bps`. Require server upper time plus one game hour strictly before quote expiry. Selection and first-send share the same complete gates, including pending controls and live yard/mortgage terms. Select annual rate then quote ID, re-evaluate and stage `/v1/loan/approve` in the same pass without donor remittances or an intermediate mission save. An approval 409 drops only that quote. Economic loss of need ends the wait and starts a six-real-hour candidate backoff; transient authority, presence, measurement or valuation gaps hold until lapse.

An unquoted lapse or definitive request refusal persists six-real-hour `_player_loan_backoffs["port|hull"]` and `_player_loan_fallback["port|hull"]` expiry timestamps, anchored to the local lapse-detection or refusal time. Even a late lapse observation gets a full six-hour ticket from detection. Goldsmiths fallback is immediately eligible for that candidate during the ticket window; the player backoff does not block it. Clear the ticket when its Goldsmiths package starts or ends. Other candidates post fresh player requests normally. Instant Goldsmiths terms remain recourse, 360-day amortised, 3,000 annual bps, minimum 10,000 pence per ship and maximum the least of 750,000 pence, 40% of quoted rebuild value and shared house funds. Request one exact quote per required lien, correcting the installment-funded principal once only, with at most two pence excess per lien. All coverage, own-cash, payback and gross debt caps still apply. Exact fallback financing refusals use `_capacity_financing_backoffs` by yard/hull; yard quote/build refusals use `_growth_build_backoffs`. These must not veto a cash-funded build or erase a winning savings target.

A player-request collateral refusal (`debt_capacity` because no priced free pledge exists or principal exceeds the highest estimated rebuild) grants the same candidate-specific six-hour Goldsmiths fallback. The instant package may use several distinct free ship liens when a single player pledge cannot fund the whole package; it still must satisfy the complete measured-net, reserve and gross-debt gates before its first draw.

Freeze admitted capital, buffer, reserve and earnings after approval. Subsequent draws and construction recheck authority, live yard/mortgage terms, fresh realized-net coverage capped at admitted earnings, debt exposure and funded capital. Build within one game day of our first approval receipt (the original action time bounds a lost response), never the lender's offer creation time. Persist that UTC deadline across restart. Expired unaccepted quotes release the package before transient hold gates. Transient authority, presence, paused clock, stale measurement or valuation holds do not cancel approved debt; economic DSCR/payback/exposure failure or the build deadline records its typed gate, backs off the candidate for six real hours and repays unused principal. Before approval, changed live mortgage terms are re-evaluated and must pass every gate; otherwise hold until lapse. An occupied slot is `mortgage_unavailable`. HTTP 401/403/429 retains unresolved intent. An unresolved request can reconcile only from exactly one own matching request created no earlier than the server-bound posting time minus five seconds; missing authority or multiple matches holds, and absent matches replay only within the normal journal window. Approval reconciliation requires the same quote ID, request ID and pledged ship. Borrowed cash cannot authorize unrelated spending while this intent is open.

A fresh complete borrower-loan snapshot and validated own-request snapshot are mandatory for borrowing and loan mutations; listing authority is also required before pledging a ship. Failed authority retains last-good regional debt reserves, not a whole-balance spending freeze: a loaded ship may still buy safety stores above those reserves, sell, deliver or dock. Missing, stale or incomplete data is never a zero-debt observation. Each active amortised loan protects `max(2 × installment_pence, next_payment_pence, arrears_pence)` in its banking region; defaulted debt and loans without a usable amortised installment protect all owed cash. Player lenders and Goldsmiths use the same servicing and surplus-prepayment rules. Automatic collection has only one game day of grace; a late amortised installment permanently defaults the whole loan.

Scheduled installments are collected automatically every ten game days. Cure active arrears ahead of discretionary work, including while unrelated actions or treasury work are pending. Fund only the loan region to `max(0, hard reserves - this loan's reserve) + arrears`, then send the cure without waiting for two future installments. Donors retain hard obligations (base reserves, berth bills, freight obligations and other debt), not ordinary cargo reservations. Do not manually retry defaulted claims; the server collects them automatically. Payment, grace and maturity timestamps are real UTC and must not be accelerated again.

Loan acceptance and repayment use immutable journal actions. An HTTP 200 receipt matching the owned loan, quote and collateral identity is authoritative even if its terms changed: record the debt, then cancel and repay an inadmissible package instead of wedging reconciliation. A matching named-loan HTTP 200 repayment is authoritative even if automatic collection changed its counters. Full early repayment omits the amount so the server clears everything owed; fund the observed owed amount plus one game day of interest. Lost-response full repayment needs a zero-owed terminal loan; a lost partial repayment can reconcile from a named-loan repayment-counter increase at least as large as the requested amount. A balance increase alone is never a loan receipt. Borrowed principal is not income; principal repayment is financing cash outflow, while interest and transfer fees remain separate costs.

`capacity_financing:<port>|<hull>` records `source`, `principal_pence`, `term_game_days`, `collateral_kind`, `ship_id`, `verdict` and the existing numeric shortfall, capacity, installment, reserve, coverage and after-interest net fields. Unknown amounts are `null`, not measured zero. `capacity_candidate:<port>|<hull>` adds `hull_category`, `evidence_basis`, `package` and `verdict`; package names mortgage principal, cash/player/Goldsmiths source, loan principal and aggregate ten-day installments. Economic verdicts include `hull`, `mortgage_unavailable`, `measured_net`, `no_depth`, `own_cash_buffer`, `dscr`, `payback`, `zone_exposure`, `debt_capacity`, `accepted_cash` and `financed`. Lifecycle refusals retain their specific request/quote/deadline gate. The researched Hamburg barque remains a worked refusal, not a promise to buy: its current occupied mortgage slot fails first; even after freeing it, own-cash buffer and the zone-exposure cap can still fail.

## Underwriting and other income

Underwriting is an executable strategy, not a display-only market. It runs after shipping, existing obligations and viable fleet investment have protected their capital. Admission currently uses a minimum 500-basis-point slice of an unchanged **hull-only** policy, excludes every company owned by the player, and requires fresh policy, ownership, cash, catalog, rate and authoritative clock observations. Missing coverage is not zero coverage. Cargo-inclusive policies remain unpriced because a hull sinking outcome does not establish their cargo-loss economics.

At least five valid closed hull-only policies on the same directed lane are required. Duplicate IDs do not increase the sample; changed terms, contradictory terminal outcomes and later nonterminal observations are censored, not counted as safe returns. A Beta(1,1) estimate uses `(losses + 1) / (closed policies + 2)`. Premium must exceed modeled claims and the opportunity cost of currently observed shipping. A fixed-look nominal 95% Wilson upper sensitivity is disclosed separately: neither estimator is a calibrated promise about hull, season, correlation or future traffic. Creation-to-last-update duration is a lock-time proxy, not a settlement deadline.

This cohort pools all recorded eras. It is **not calibrated to the announced cargo-value targeting and ship-capture rules**. The published contract has no reliable deployment boundary or historical cargo, gun, convoy and zone observations with which to reconstruct a comparable post-patch cohort. A hull-only declaration does not prove that a foreign ship is empty or that its whole cargo was declared. The positive-net decision therefore remains a bounded model-based pilot, not measured post-patch expected profit; it must not justify increasing the slice or the permanent loss allowance.

Reserve the full possible collateral loss. The permanent pilot starts at 10% of uncommitted cash; active collateral and realized losses consume it, and only 10% of positive settled underwriting net replenishes it. It does not reset on restart. Before the debit region is known, the slice must fit protected cash in the home region, policy-origin region and every other capable vault. After the first accepted slice, further underwriting waits for a unique, identity-linked collateral debit to an owned vault. An equal-sized unrelated transfer is not proof. Actual vault cash already excludes escrow; do not deduct accepted collateral from that cash a second time.

The immutable 200 response must identify one exact owned slice with present, bounded collateral and premium amounts. Membership or aggregate coverage cannot reconstruct a lost receipt. Preserve ambiguous requests under their original key while unrelated safe fleet work continues. Premium remains an entitlement until an unchanged terminal hull-policy observation establishes earned premium and claims. Safe closure returns principal; loss closure consumes collateral. Net is earned premium minus claims and fees, never collateral returned plus premium. No undocumented insurance fee or voluntary release endpoint is assumed.

### Covered instruments and underworld observations

The [marque controller](../internal/engine/marque.go) automatically assesses 500 bps issues for 60 game hours, with at most one active marque. Require at least one laden passage per game day, no pricing floor, zone total at most 5,000 bps, estimated prize after the Admiral's tenth at least 1.5 times cost, and cost at most 5% of liquid funds. Lifetime expired cost minus prizes plus new cost must not exceed 300,000 pence (3,000 Cr). A fresh pre-send quote cannot cost more than the admitted quote and must pass every rule again; the game still has no atomic maximum-price field.

For each zone, use at most the latest six expiries within 30 game days. Once there are at least three, total prize must cover total cost, not each issue individually. A negative zone enters a seven-game-day backoff, then may make one qualifying probe. An active or unresolved probe cannot admit another issue; if its completion leaves the zone window negative, a new seven-game-day backoff starts before any reissue. Re-reading the same outcome does not keep extending the deadline. Globally, use at most the latest ten expiries within 60 game days. Below total prize of 1.05 times total cost, a fresh quote must estimate at least twice cost instead of the normal 1.5 times. These are temporary economic controls, not lifetime loss-count stops. Windows and backoffs use authoritative server time and its game-time scale.

The backoff retains the rolling window that earned it. A new loss through window roll-out, a corrected final prize or a completed probe is reassessed even when the newest expiry is unchanged. An unchanged negative window cannot move the deadline; a corrected nonnegative window releases its economic backoff without removing a separate domain-refusal wait. Only a list requested after expiry establishes a final prize.

A price increase proven to have prevented sending retires the frozen admission and persists a thirty-real-minute retry delay, matching the cached quote lifetime. Ship work continues during that delay. Any later purchase needs a fresh quote and all normal admission checks; a pending or ambiguous issue request is never retired by this rule.

Never sponsor a zone used by our ships, persisted routes, occupied ports, home port or an underwritten voyage. Missing exposure authority fails closed. Active and pending marque zones constrain new voyages. Existing paid cargo is not stranded merely to reapply this discretionary admission rule. Estimated prize is not earned income. `_marque_outcomes` retains cost, prize, sea zone and expiry; legacy entries keep their lifetime accounting and gain zone/time evidence when an authoritative letter is merged. `_marque_active` retains active exposure and `_marque_backoffs` retains zone deadlines. `_marque_stopped` remains a manual owner kill-switch and is never auto-set from losses. At deployment the integrator clears the live legacy value left by the retired three-loss latch once, preserving financial history, active exposure, pending actions and backoffs. Later explicit manual stops remain honored.

The [futures controller](../internal/engine/futures.go) runs automatically at accessible hubs without an admission environment toggle, fixed Amsterdam/commodity cohort, one-ton admission rule or operator re-arm command. It first services mature obligations and recovery, then compares warehouse-covered sales into player bids and purchase-before-sale crosses accepted atomically. Own quotes, incoming ship coverage and downstream ship purchases are retired. Futures never hold a ship, redirect it or move its cargo. A cross needs the same commodity, hub and lot size and distinct counterparties. Its purchase must mature at least one game hour before its sale, delivering into our leased warehouse to cover the sale without opening stock. After a delayed pass, maturity ordering services the purchase first.

Standalone sales reserve unlocked owned warehouse stock net of other obligations and terminal stock awaiting liquidation. A cross purchase reserves receiving space, full strike cash, buyer customs and margin; both legs' margin carry and credit allowances plus rent reduce estimated net. Missing or stale books, cash, warehouse or clock evidence cannot authorize a new commitment. Only foreign-player listings qualify: every company in the fresh player profile is excluded, and usable counterparty credit evidence remains required.

Receiving space is reserved across every commodity at a port. An admitted cross may journal an exact warehouse lease increase before its immutable atomic acceptance; unused controller-owned capacity is released without shrinking the original lease or occupied stock. If capacity or the admitted lease target disappears before acceptance is attempted, the unsent group is rejected rather than blocking later admissions. The 50-pence-per-ton-game-day rent allowance is a model based on the measured Amsterdam tariff, not an authoritative worldwide rent quote.

Terminal net purchase stock, including purchases whose old consuming mission disappeared, is liquidated through journaled one-ton IOC sales at the NPC bid. Exact settlement receipts and durable per-cohort exit totals prevent double sales across restart. Unknown stock trades retain their original key and inventory baseline; fresh warehouse inventory and unique private fills can reconcile physical delivery without inventing cash. Used stock and unresolved receiving reservations keep their lease protected until exit.

Size the book from at most 10% of free regional cash after operating and mission commitments, with at most 25% of that book assigned to one counterparty. The 2,000,000-pence limit (20,000 Cr) is a final sanity bound, not the sizing target. An estimated opportunity must beat its executable spot alternative by at least the larger of 100 pence or 5%, after customs, rent allowance, margin carry and counterparty credit stress. A usable report carries credit stress of at least 5% of notional, rising to 25% for historical defaults or claims; missing/stale reports and open claims do not qualify. These are deliberately conservative model allowances, not calibrated probabilities. The local simulator has no futures model, so there is no simulated futures P&L or historical benchmark promise behind admission.

Futures and exchange SSE events invalidate the futures list; only own cash-changing events fence fleet financial authority. REST and validated receipts remain authoritative. An hourly real-time backstop replaces per-pass list polling, with maturity wakes and authoritative refresh before mutations. A successful `settled` stream receipt may omit default-only penalty and claim fields, which then mean zero. A `defaulted` receipt must explicitly provide both, even when zero, and all evidence must match immutable contract identity, parties, physical tons and available signed cash. A terminal list row without its exact receipt is recovered through journaled settlement; missing list rows do not resolve obligations. Margin returns, principal and sale proceeds alone are not profit. Realized cohort P&L needs complete contract cash and physical-stock cost basis; unpriced opening stock, inventory-only trade cash and unmeasured rent remain unknown.

The retired Amsterdam lease and stock wind down before that legacy mission can become `futures_done`, including when no new admission is profitable. Done requires the lease to return to `InitialLease` or below with no owned excess stock left unaccounted for. An unavailable legacy exit does not disable unrelated controller opportunities, and its remaining stock cannot cover a new sale. Closing the observed 2 t lease avoids an estimated 1 Cr per game day of recurring rent at the recorded tariff; this is a cost-saving estimate, not a recurring futures-trading profit forecast.

Transient never-sent clock, market or funding failures retain immutable admission terms, persist a retry cooldown and let ordinary ship work continue. Immutable-term or cap violations stop that admission; positive no-send proof releases its reservations. An ambiguous accept can also resolve as rejected when a matching listing from a GET begun after the observation fence remains open or names another acceptor. The fence is the later of reconciliation time or the last transport completion plus the 45-second request timeout and a five-second margin; absent attempt timing starts the wait at reconciliation. One such leg proves an atomic batch did not commit at observation time. Missing, stale or changed listings do not prove rejection, and no expired request is resent. Durable negative proof survives a crash before mission clearing, but later exact listings naming us as a party re-adopt rejected intents with their original coverage. Final settlement receipts remain terminal. A 403 admission refusal rejects that listing without stopping fleet execution. Other unknown actions retain their original request and idempotency key. Default liability clears only when validated post-default own credit reports zero open futures claims. A matched penalty debit identifies our default but does not discharge a separate unpaid claim. Last validated positive aggregate claims remain reserved until a later valid reduction, with conservative extra coverage for newer defaults. Own credit reads are durable and limited to once per five real minutes while claims remain unresolved, separately from rival lookup budgets.

The [underworld observer](../internal/engine/underworld.go) is observe-only. `underworld-key` creates a separate encrypted company key with exactly `read` and `underworld` scopes. The scope can authorize game mutations, but the observer interface exposes only single-attempt GETs. Each stationary port visit gets at most one wants read; a cove visit also gets at most four contraband-book reads. Reservations survive failures and restart. Missing credentials, HTTP 403 with code `missing_scope`, or observer storage/payload failures disable the observer for that process without stopping trading. Other 403s skip only that read, not the whole observer. Ordinary trade never buys or runs contraband. No smuggling income or unseen buyers enter the planner.

The bulletin is public deal talk signed with the company name ([guide section 3.21](https://tradewinds.kibb.io/skill.md)): a post is permanent ten real minutes after posting and a thread archives after three quiet real days. The runner posts at most once every twelve real hours, well inside the game limit of 10 posts and 3 new threads per company and real hour. It alternates between its own thread and the latest unreplied Kraken Cartel thread that is neither archived nor removed, and falls back to its own thread when no such thread exists.

The voice is a curated, friendly Pokemon pirate that openly supports Kraken Cartel expansion, freight and the forward book. Posts contain no authenticated fleet census, ship labels, vault balances, reserves, delivery locations or exact trading plans. The bulletin is public and the server enforces nothing agreed there.

Sending is idle-only journal work at the tail of a pass and never blocks trading or futures work. Company posting cooldowns use the later usable Retry-After or detail time, falling back to one hour for `429 bulletin_limit` or 24 hours for `403 posting_muted`. True global limits remain shared, authorization failures stop execution, and other 4xx refusals wait six hours. Schedule, template cursors and bounded public-page scan progress persist across restarts. Ambiguous requests retain their immutable payload and key; expired requests can reconcile from later pages but are never resent.

## Durable economic record

Keep settled mission economics beyond the seven-day diagnostic window. Acquisition basis, actual proceeds, fees, provisions, and attributable operating costs must survive restarts and pruning. Journal each upstream mutation once and reconcile ambiguous results before recording profit. Transfers, seed capital, borrowed principal, collateral principal, expansion bonuses and unrealized inventory changes are not trading profit. Bonus receipts may explain a net-worth change but never establish repeatable revenue.

Working snapshots retain current requested child datasets and their metadata, not every historically visited market or route origin. A currently required child fetch failure retains its last-good value with explicit stale/error metadata. Durable journals, settlements, and retained diagnostics remain separate from this bounded working state.

Schema 21 stores route payloads in account-scoped `snapshot_routes` rows with a separate 64 MiB per-account cap on the canonical encoded route object, including keys, escaping and numeric expansion. Route freshness in `_meta` remains in the 16 MiB working snapshot and commits atomically with route changes; extraction does not remove that remaining metadata budget. An invalid route capture preserves the last good entry and marks stale/error metadata. The existing route-history count and byte caps are unchanged. Replay still bounds aggregate plaintext frames at 16 MiB and can omit the largest datasets, including routes, with explicit gaps rather than preserving every operational cache entry. This storage change does not alter route legality, freshness or economic admission policy. Follow the [schema 21 cutover procedure](runbook.md#route-cache-schema-21-cutover) for backup and compatible recovery.

Startup logs and skips incomplete registrations, unreadable credential rows, or missing API keys per account. Those failures do not stop healthy account workers or the read-only dashboard API.
