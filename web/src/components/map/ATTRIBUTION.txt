The GeoJSON is bundled locally and is not fetched from the source at runtime. The chart uses no remote tile service. This generalized coastline is context, not a navigation aid.

Port coordinates in lib/geography.ts are approximate WGS84 harbour/city coordinates. Sea-zone centres and straight connections between recorded route nodes are schematic estimates, not recorded tracks. Sailing positions interpolate only the current recorded leg using its real UTC departure and arrival timestamps. Clock and ship observation age, pause state, unknown endpoints, and missing metadata can prevent or freeze an estimate. Reaching a scheduled ETA never confirms arrival.
